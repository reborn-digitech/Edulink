# 08 · Remaining risks and owner decisions

Audit of 5 October 2026. These are the larger product ideas, rules to choose and risks that are **not fixes**: nothing here is in `02-findings.md` as a row to close, and no fix builder changes them. Each is traced to the report that raised it, with an owner and a recommended option. Where the report itself gave a recommendation it is quoted as such; where it did not, the option is marked "(writer's reading)" and is the least-change choice, to be confirmed by the owner. Owner key, as in the 4 October gap analysis: **Owner** = the product owner; **Counsel**; **Pilot** = a pilot college's named office; **Next builder** = an engineering task with no product choice left.

Sources: this audit's layers (`01-inventory.md`, `03-security.md`, `04-journeys.md`, `05-states.md`), `scratchpad/reports/streams-s4.md`, `streams-s4b-fixes.md`, `streams-sweep.md`, `streams-sweep-fixes.md`, `insights-engine.md`, and `scratchpad/campaign2/gaps.md` section 7 (the 34 open decisions of 4 October).
