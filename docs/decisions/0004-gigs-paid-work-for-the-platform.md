# 0004 — Gigs: work done for the platform, and what each pupil gets free

Status: accepted (2026-09-30), amended 2026-10-02 twice (paid seats, then the company as the collecting party; see the two amendments below — the second supersedes the first's money path). Governs `apps/web/src/lib/gig-questions.ts`, `gig-sessions.ts`, `gig-payments.ts`, `gig-gateway.ts` and `gig-invoice-pdf.tsx`, the `Gig*`, `QuestionContribution`, `ExpertPayoutAccount`, `SessionPayment`, `ExpertPayout` and `GigInvoice` tables, and the `/api/v1/gigs`, `/api/v1/expert/payout`, `/api/v1/expert/earnings`, `/api/v1/expert/invoices`, `/api/v1/appadmin/contributions` and `/api/v1/operator/gigs` areas. Decision 0003 is unchanged and still governs school fees.

## Decision

Experts and teachers may do work through the platform that is first given free and then paid. Every pupil gets a free allowance; past it a guardian pays, and the company keeps a commission. The owner chose the following on 2026-09-30, from options put with their arithmetic (the numbers are section 9 of the pricing document, `pricing-tiers-v3` + gigs).

## What is built, and what is not

**Built (2026-09-30): questions for the bank.** `QuestionContribution` (the question in three languages, SUBMITTED → RETURNED / ACCEPTED / REJECTED / WITHDRAWN), `GigEarning` (written once, when a question is added; PAID or FREE_BLOCK), `GigPayment` (the operator's record of a transfer, by its UTR), `GigPayee` (a teacher's own UPI id) and `GigSettings` (the six dials). A teacher writes at `/teacher/contributions`; the content team reads at `/appadmin/contributions`; the operator holds the dials and records payments at `/root/gigs`.

**Built (2026-09-30): the free tier of sessions.** `GigSlot` (a time a provider offers: an expert's one-to-one or workshop for every school's pupils, a teacher's one-to-one for their own school's, on the provider's own https meeting link) and `GigBooking` (a pupil's seat: PENDING_CONSENT → CONFIRMED → DELIVERED / MISSED / UNHELD, or CANCELLED), `src/lib/gig-sessions.ts`. A pupil or a guardian books at `/sessions`; an expert offers at `/expert/sessions` and a teacher at `/teacher/guidance`; the office reads and cancels at `/admin/sessions`, where it also switches teachers' guidance on; the operator holds nine more dials at `/root/gigs`. What was decided in building it, beyond the table above:

- A pupil's own ask waits for a guardian; a guardian's booking for their child is confirmed at once. The school does not approve a session, but its office is told when an outside adult is to meet one of its pupils, reads every booking, and may cancel any with a reason.
- The meeting link is shown to the pupil and their guardians only while the seat is confirmed, and to the office never.
- A teacher's guidance draws on the same two one-to-ones a year as an expert's, since that is the arithmetic the allowance was set by.
- A provider's free duty is counted and shown to them; nothing yet enforces it, there being nothing yet to charge for.
- A confirmed seat in a session that starts within a day is reminded once to the pupil and their guardians, and the provider once for the slot, by the hourly job `gigs:remind`.

**Built (2026-10-02): paid workshop seats** — see the first amendment below for the rule of who may charge, the second for how the money travels, and `apps/web/src/lib/gig-payments.ts` for the money.

**On the phone (2026-09-30).** Sessions is one route for every role: a pupil or a guardian books and answers, an expert (for whom it is a fourth tab) or a teacher offers, marks and cancels, and the office reads, cancels and turns the guidance switch. A teacher's questions are two screens, the list with their standing and where they are paid, and the question itself in three languages. The content team's review and the operator's dials and payments stay on the web.

## The invariants of what is built

1. **Only adding to the bank earns**, and only a member of the content team or the operator adds. The bank's row, its two translations, the contribution's status and the earning are one transaction (`decideContribution`).
2. **The limits are counted under a lock** on the settings row, so two reviewers adding at once cannot take a teacher past their month or the month past its budget (`tests/gig-questions.test.ts` adds two at once and expects one).
3. **The platform pays nobody.** A `GigPayment` is the operator's record of a transfer made from the company's bank outside the app. Its amount is the server's sum of the earnings it settles, never a typed one; a reference is recorded once; only months that have ended are settled. No gateway call exists in this area, and decision 0003's invariants test still passes untouched.
4. **The person is the fence.** These tables carry no school: the bank is every school's, the reviewer is the content team and the payer is the company. A teacher reads and changes only their own questions; another teacher's is a 404 (the cross-person probe tries it). A teacher's UPI id is read by that teacher and by the operator, and is never written to the audit log.
5. **A pupil's allowance and a slot's seats are counted where they cannot be raced.** A booking is one transaction under a lock on the pupil's own membership row: the year's seats are counted, the seat is claimed by an update that cannot take a slot past its seats, and the booking is written (`bookSeat`; `tests/gig-sessions.test.ts` sends three pupils after two seats and two asks after a pupil's last free one).
6. **A minor does not book alone, and an outside adult reaches only the seat.** An expert reads their own bookings by their own user id and nothing of the school through them (`npm run rls:verify` asserts it); what they write to one the server writes under the school's binding after finding the booking to be theirs.
7. **An import does not delete a teacher's work.** `quiz:import` removes from a chapter only the questions a file wrote; one made in the app (`source` "Shalaghar content team" or "teacher contribution", the constants in `src/lib/bank-source.ts`; "Relay content team" until 2 October 2026, rewritten by the migration `bank_source_shalaghar`) is left.

## Amendment of 2 October 2026 — paid seats, and mentoring as the price of charging

The owner decided on 2 October 2026 to build paid sessions now, behind an env switch and the devbox transport, the lawyer's opinion and the company's gateway account being the owner's to obtain. The rule that governs it, and what it changes in the table above:

- **One-to-one mentoring is always free, and is the condition of charging, not the thing charged for.** The ₹150–600 one-to-one price line is struck: a one-to-one slot can never carry a price (`offerSlot` refuses it with `gigs.refuseOneToOnePriced`), whoever offers it.
- **An expert may price a workshop seat only while** (a) their KYC is VERIFIED (`expert-kyc.ts` is the one truth), (b) they have an ACTIVE payout account — their own linked account at the company's gateway, linked after KYC — (c) they keep an open free one-to-one offer (a `GigSlot` or `ExpertSlot` window of kind one-to-one, free, in the coming 30 days), and (d) they have delivered the period's free one-to-ones per the free-duty counters. **The free duty becomes "keeps an open free mentoring offer and has delivered their free one-to-ones for the period"**; the free workshop quota stays asked of the expert and shown to them, and no longer gates a price. Each condition refuses by name (`paidGate` in `gig-sessions.ts`), and the expert's sessions page shows the four as a checklist.
- **A workshop seat is ₹50–300** (`GigSettings.seatPriceMinPaise`/`seatPriceMaxPaise`, the operator's dials) with **15 % commission** (`commissionPercent`), the gateway's fee paid out of the commission.
- **A pupil's free allowance is used first.** Past it a guardian pays at booking; a pupil's own ask on a paid seat waits for the guardian's consent AND payment (`PENDING_CONSENT` → `PENDING_PAYMENT` → `CONFIRMED`). An unpaid seat is given back after half an hour by the job.
- ~~**The money travels as decision 0003 would have it for the platform's own account:**~~ *(superseded the same day by the second amendment below; kept as written)* one order at the company's Razorpay account carrying a Route transfer of the seat price less the commission to the expert's linked account, `on_hold`; the hold is released by the hourly job `gigs:release` once the seat is DELIVERED (or MISSED) plus 48 hours, grouped into one `ExpertPayout` per expert per run, the gateway's settlement id recorded when its webhook names it; free cancellation to 12 hours before (a full refund through the gateway with the transfer reversed); an expert's no-show (UNHELD) refunds in full; a pupil's no-show (MISSED) and a late cancellation pay the expert. Every state change is an audit row; the pupil's school's office is told as it is for a free seat.
- **The kill switch** `GigSettings.paymentsEnabled`, the operator's on `/root/gigs`, is read by every paid path: off, no price is stored, no order made, no hold released; refunds still go through. On a deployed server with no `GATEWAY_PLATFORM_KEY_ID` the transport is "off" and the same holds.
- **The company's gateway account** is `GATEWAY_PLATFORM_KEY_ID`, `GATEWAY_PLATFORM_SECRET_ENC` (sealed under `FEES_SECRET_KEY` by `npm run secret:seal`) and `GATEWAY_PLATFORM_WEBHOOK_SECRET`, read in `gig-gateway-env.ts` and spoken to only by `gig-gateway.ts`, whose own allow-list is the six Route calls (an order with a transfer, a payment's transfers, a transfer's release, a refund with reversal, a linked account made and read). `gateway.ts` and `tests/fees-invariants.test.ts` are untouched: school fees never come here.
- **Untested against a real account:** the Route linked-account onboarding (what `POST /v2/accounts` answers, whether an onboarding page is returned, which webhook says "activated") and the real transfer/settlement webhooks. Everything is exercised under the devbox transport and the fake webhook signature (`tests/gig-payments.test.ts`).

## Second amendment of 2 October 2026 — the company collects, invoices its commission, and pays the expert itself

The owner decided later on 2 October 2026, superseding the gateway-split money path of the first amendment (which landed that morning as c2b621c5): **the company is the collecting party and the merchant of record for its commission.** The guardian pays the company; the company keeps its share and pays the expert from its own bank. No Route, no linked accounts, no transfers. The owner's reasons: one party on the receipt the family holds, one party the expert is invoiced by and paid from, and the split a matter of the company's own books rather than of a gateway product whose onboarding, settlement and reversal webhooks had never been seen against a real account. Who may charge (the first amendment's gate: KYC, a way to be paid, an open free one-to-one, the period's free one-to-ones given) is unchanged. What changed:

- **Collection.** `openCheckout` makes a plain order on the company's gateway account for the full seat price (`gig-gateway.ts` may now make exactly three calls: an order, a payment read, a refund; the webhook handles `payment.captured`, `payment.failed` and `refund.failed` and nothing else). `markCaptured` is unchanged in effect — PAID, the seat CONFIRMED, one tenant transaction — and now also freezes the figures and takes the receipt number.
- **Deductions, frozen at capture on the `SessionPayment` row:** `amountPaise` the gross; `commissionPaise` = `GigSettings.commissionPercent` (15) of the gross; `gstPaise` = `GigSettings.gstPercent` (18) of the commission; `netPaise` = gross − commission − GST, the one stored copy of a derived value here, with one writer and a `data:audit` check (`gig-money`). The gateway's fee is the company's, out of the commission, as before. The expert sees the three figures on their earnings page and on the invoice; the family sees the gross alone.
- **Hold, then due.** Statuses `INITIATED | PAID | HELD | DUE | PAID_OUT | REFUNDED | FAILED` (DUE and PAID_OUT replace RELEASED). The hold is still delivered or missed plus 48 hours (a late family cancellation: the slot's end plus 48 hours). The hourly job `gigs:release` moves HELD rows past their hold to DUE and attaches each to the expert's one OPEN `ExpertPayout` (`netPaise` summed, `paymentsCount`), opening it if they have none and telling the expert once when it opens. Nothing moves to DUE while the kill switch is off; refunds still go.
- **Payout by the company.** `ExpertPayoutAccount` is now the expert's payee details, typed on the "Get paid" card: a payee name; a UPI id, or a bank account sealed by `secret-box.ts` under the binding `gig:payee:<expertUserId>` with its IFSC (the last four digits kept in the clear, the sealed number never returned by any read); the two-digit GST state code (the place of supply on the invoice); a GSTIN if they have one (the invoice is then B2B). ACTIVE when the details are complete AND KYC is VERIFIED, read fresh each time; the operator may SUSPEND with a note, and `paidGate.payoutActive` reads this. On `/root/gigs` the operator sees each OPEN payout with the expert, the payee name, the UPI id or the masked account and IFSC, the net and the seat count, pays it from the company's bank app, and records the transfer (`POST /operator/gigs/payouts/{payoutId}/pay`: the UTR, the date, a note) — which marks the payout PAID and every seat in it PAID_OUT, issues the invoice, audits `gig.payout.paid`, and tells the expert. The same on the phone's operator payouts screen.
- **The GST tax invoice.** One `GigInvoice` per payout, issued in the transaction that records the payout: `number` from a financial-year series `<prefix>/<FY>/<0001>` (`GigSettings.invoicePrefix`, "SG" to begin) taken under the `GigSettings` row lock inside that same transaction, so a number is never reused and a failed write leaves no gap; `taxableValuePaise` = the commission on the payout's seats; CGST+SGST (the GST halved) when the expert's state is the company's, IGST otherwise; the seller (`companyLegalName`, `companyGstin`, `companyAddress`, `companyStateCode` on `GigSettings`, editable on `/root/gigs`) and the buyer frozen as JSON; the SAC (`invoiceSacCode`, default 998599 — **the accountant must confirm it**); one line per seat. **A payout cannot be recorded until the company's legal name, GSTIN and state code are set** (`gigs.refuseCompanyDetails`, 422; the card names what is missing). The PDF is rendered on read (`gig-invoice-pdf.tsx`, @react-pdf with the bundled Mukta face) in the expert's own language at issue, titled "Tax Invoice", with the number, date, the seller's GSTIN and address, the buyer, the place of supply, the SAC, the taxable value, the tax split, the total in figures and words, and the sentence that the amount was deducted from the seat fees collected on the expert's behalf. Doors: `GET /expert/invoices`, `GET /expert/invoices/{invoiceId}/pdf` (the expert's own, 404 for another's; `budget()` like every PDF), `GET /operator/gigs/invoices`.
- **The family's receipt.** `receiptNumber` on the `SessionPayment` row, series `<prefix>-R/<FY>/<n>` under the same lock, taken at capture; `GET /gigs/payments/{paymentId}/receipt` (PARENT, and a STUDENT for their own seat) renders it in the reader's language: the gross, the payer, the pupil, the seat, the expert, the gateway's payment id, "collected by <company> on behalf of <expert>", and the refund line when REFUNDED. Linked from `/sessions` and the phone.
- **Refunds** are as before: the whole gross back through the gateway from the company's balance; a refunded seat's commission, GST and net are zeroed and it never joins a payout; a failed refund waits for the job or the operator's retry (`POST /operator/gigs/payments/{paymentId}/retry` — moved from under `/payouts/`, since Next.js cannot hold `{paymentId}` and `{payoutId}` in the same position).
- **Migration** `20261002100602_gig_company_collects` (+ `20261002100826_gig_payment_paid_at`): the new columns and the `GigInvoice` table; `ExpertPayout.amountPaise`→`netPaise`, `transfers`→`paymentsCount`, `SessionPayment.releasedAt`→`dueAt` renamed so nothing is lost; RELEASED rows → PAID_OUT and their payouts PAID with a note; the Route columns (`linkedAccountId`, `onboardingUrl`, `transferId`, `settlementId`) dropped — nothing read them once the adapter lost the calls.

**Questions still for the accountant** (the owner's to put; nothing here is settled by code):

1. **TCS under section 52 of the CGST Act.** The company now collects the consideration for a supply (the expert's workshop) made through its platform. If it is an electronic commerce operator in the Act's sense, it must collect tax at source (1 %) on the net value of the expert's supplies, register for it, file GSTR-8, and the expert must be registered to take the credit. The code deducts no TCS today; `netPaise` would need a fourth figure.
2. **The SAC.** 998599 ("other support services n.e.c.") is a placeholder for the commission; 998314 or a marketplace-facilitation heading may fit better. One code for the series, set before the first real invoice.
3. **The point of taxation.** The invoice is issued when the payout is recorded (the commission is "earned" once the seat is delivered and the hold has run). Whether the time of supply is the capture, the delivery or the payout decides which month's GSTR-1 carries it; today's code dates the invoice at the payout.
4. **The family's receipt when the expert is GST-registered.** The receipt names the company as collecting on the expert's behalf and shows no tax. If a registered expert's workshop is itself a taxable supply, the family's document may need the expert's GSTIN and tax — which would make it the expert's invoice, not the company's receipt.
5. **Whether the commission's GST should be charged on the gross commission or net of the gateway's fee**, and whether the gateway's own invoice to the company is creditable — a bookkeeping question, not a product one.

Decision 0003 is untouched: a school's fees still never touch the platform, `tests/fees-invariants.test.ts` still holds `gateway.ts` to four calls and the schema to no Platform/Wallet/Escrow/Nodal/Settlement/Payout model, and the gig money stays in its own modules under its own names.

**Untested against a real account:** every call runs under the devbox transport and the fake webhook signature (`tests/gig-payments.test.ts`); the invoice's wording has not been read by an accountant.
