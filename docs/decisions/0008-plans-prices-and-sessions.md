# 0008 — Plans, prices, one-time practice, and sessions booked per session

Status: accepted by the owner (7 November 2026), from the owner's decisions of 7 November 2026. Amends decision 0006 in three places (the TEACHER plan's contents, the price list, the company's share) and keeps the rest of 0006 and 0004. Governs the `PersonPlan` rows' prices, `PLAN_FEATURES.TEACHER` (`packages/contracts/src/v1/person-plans.ts`), `GigSettings.commissionPercent` and the four `freeLimit*Minutes` dials, `User.premiumMeeting`, the cap in `apps/web/src/lib/gig-sessions.ts` (`assertMeetingCap`, `meetingCapFor` in `meeting-links.ts`), the door `POST /api/v1/gigs/meeting-account`, the one-time practice (`apps/web/src/lib/practice-once.ts`, the `PracticeOnce` table, the `/api/v1/practice/once` doors) and session series (`GigSeries`, `GigSlot.seriesId`, the `/api/v1/gigs/series` doors). Decisions 0002 (minors) and 0003 (school fees) are unchanged and applied here.

## The owner's decisions

1. **The pupil (STUDENT) plan stays** — the assistant, the question bank, the platform's competitions — at ₹99 a month, ₹999 a year.
2. **The TEACHER and EXPERT plans are priced like the pupil plan**: ₹99 a month, ₹999 a year. (0006 left every price to the operator; the dev rows carried ₹199/₹1,999 for a teacher and ₹499/₹4,999 for an expert.)
3. **The TEACHER plan includes the assistant, the question bank and sessions**, beside the question gig and the job board that 0006 gave it and the owner has not withdrawn. A teacher may set bank work for any pupil; **a pupil without the plan opens an assigned set once**, scored, as a one-time practice, and meets the plan card for the bank itself, the journey and revision. The one-time set counts toward nothing else: no mastery, no journey, no badge.
4. **The EXPERT plan's features are as they were** (EXPERT_SESSIONS); the session rules below apply to an expert as to a teacher.
5. **Sessions, for a teacher and an expert alike**: a single session, a series (a fixed set of dated sessions) or a scheduled recurring session (a weekly pattern until a date). **No monthly pass and no standing access**: a guardian books each session for a pupil under eighteen (a college student of eighteen or more books for themselves), the way a seat is bought today; "book the remaining sessions of this series" is one order of individual seats, each a booking the guardian chose. The meeting link shows after payment and consent, as today.
6. **The platform's share is 20 percent of every paid seat**, a teacher's or an expert's, replacing 0004's 15.
7. **The meeting room is the provider's own** Google Meet, Zoom, Teams or Jitsi link (`meeting-links.ts`, as 0006 built it). A provider says once whether their account is premium; without that, a session's length is capped at the service's free limit — a setting per service, defaults Zoom 40 minutes, Meet 60, Teams 60, Jitsi none — and the page says so when the time is set. Verifying accounts is not built.
8. **The minors' rule (0002) stands**: the guardian buys, the link shows only after purchase and consent, and the provider's identity check applies to a teacher as it does to an expert today.

## How it is built (first commit, 7 November 2026: the record, the prices, the share, the cap)

- **The price list is a constant and a migration.** `PLAN_PRICE_PAISE = { MONTH: 9900, YEAR: 99900 }` in the contracts; the data migration `20261107091000_plan_prices_decision_0008` writes it onto all three `PersonPlan` rows whatever stood (the owner's decision fixes the list; the operator's page `/root/features` remains the way to change a row later, and the card then shows what the row says), and `person-plans.ts` seeds a missing row with it rather than with 0. `active` is untouched: a plan is asked for only when the operator makes it active, as 0006 says.
- **The TEACHER plan holds the question bank.** `PLAN_FEATURES.TEACHER = ASSISTANT, QUESTION_BANK, QUESTION_GIG, TEACHER_SESSIONS, JOB_BOARD`; the same migration appends `QUESTION_BANK` to the TEACHER row's list, from which grants and passes write their entitlements. The plan card (web and phone) now also says in one sentence what each plan includes (`plans.includesStudent/Teacher/Expert`), under the feature names.
- **The share.** `GigSettings.commissionPercent` defaults to 20 (schema) and the migration moves the one row from 15 to 20 — only from 15, so a figure the operator had set by hand would be theirs. The computation is unchanged (`gig-payments.ts` `figuresOf`: the commission, GST on the commission, the net to the provider), and every `SessionPayment` keeps the figures it was made under; nothing already frozen is recomputed. The fixtures and the suites carry the new figures (₹100 gross → ₹20, ₹3.60, ₹76.40).
- **The cap.** Four dials on `GigSettings` — `freeLimitZoomMinutes` 40, `freeLimitMeetMinutes` 60, `freeLimitTeamsMinutes` 60, `freeLimitJitsiMinutes` 0 — on the operator's sessions card at `/root/gigs`, 0 meaning no cap, held to 0–180 (a session is 30–180 minutes, so a cap above 180 holds nothing). `meetingCapFor(link, caps, premium)` is pure: the service the link is a room of (the same `meetingServiceOf` that admits the link), its cap, and nothing when the provider is premium, the service has no cap or the link is not a room. `offerSlot` refuses a longer session with `seats.refuseFreeLimit`, naming the service and its minutes; the offer form says the same sentence beside the minutes once the link is typed (`capSentence`, over the same function, so the page and the server cannot disagree). A one-to-one is thirty minutes and inside every default cap; the cap bites a workshop and (third commit) a series's sessions.
- **The premium switch is on the person, not on a profile.** `User.premiumMeeting`, said once by `POST /api/v1/gigs/meeting-account` (an expert or a teacher, their own row, audited `gig.meeting.premium`, a repeat writing nothing) and shown as one checkbox on the offering page beside the form, with the note that nobody checks it and that a session the service cuts short is the provider's to answer for. The brief named "ExpertProfile and the teacher's equivalent"; a teacher has no profile row of their own, and the room is the person's whichever school or kind they offer under, so one column on `User` read by one function (`meetingAccountOf`) serves both — the builder's call, to be confirmed. An alumnus mentor's free one-to-one (`placement-alumni.ts`) is thirty minutes and is not asked.
- **Not built, as decided:** verifying a provider's account with the service; the cap on the phone's offer form (the phone reads `meeting` on `GET /api/v1/gigs/slots` and the server refuses the same way; the sentence beside the time is the web's).

## How it is built (second commit, 7 November 2026: one-time practice)

- **One row, one writer.** `PracticeOnce` (`apps/web/src/lib/practice-once.ts`): one per pupil and assignment — the drawn question ids in serving order, the answers in order with their verdicts, the counts, `finishedAt`. A school's row under RLS like every practice row. The first open fixes the draw: the chapter's served questions nearest the assignment's target rung, the lower rung first on a tie, id order within a rung (pickQuestion's own order), ten of them (`ONCE_QUESTIONS`; fewer when the chapter holds fewer; a chapter with nothing served is refused `practiceOnce.refuseEmpty`). Two opens at once meet the unique index and the second reads the first's row.
- **Each question once, the set once.** An answer is recorded by a conditional update on the answers as they were read, so a double tap or the outbox's second delivery gets the stored verdict (`replayed`) and moves nothing twice; an out-of-range choice is recorded wrong, never refused. The verdict comes back with the working, as the bank's does, and the next question; the last answer stamps `finishedAt` and the score. A finished set opens on its result with no question. There is no reveal (no badge to forfeit) and no second attempt.
- **Counts toward nothing else.** No `QuizAttempt`, no `QuizResponse`, no `QuizMastery`, no badge, no journey, no weekly count: the test asserts the rows stay at zero. The teacher's class view carries the one-time score beside the rung (`once` on each pupil row: correct, total, answered, finished), so a class of pupils without the plan does not read as "never opened".
- **The doors are plan-free, not plan-checked.** `GET /api/v1/practice/once` (the sets with where each stands), `POST /api/v1/practice/once` (open or read back), `POST /api/v1/practice/once/answer` — STUDENT, feature QUESTION_BANK with `planFree`, so the platform switch alone gates them (off is 404) and the handler decides nothing about the plan. A pupil who holds the plan may call them and gets the same one-time set; the pages draw them only for a locked pupil, and a pupil with the plan plays the chapter through `/attempts`, where the assignment's rung is proven. The builder's reading: refusing a plan-holder would make the door's answer depend on the plan the sweep switches on and off, and nothing is gained by it.
- **Where a locked pupil meets it.** The web's practice layout keeps drawing the plan card in place of every page under `/student/practice`, and under it the sets a teacher assigned (`OnceSetsCard`, each with Open once / Continue / See the result); the set's own page is `/student/practice-once/{assignmentId}`, outside that layout so the card never covers it, gated by the switch alone (`requireFeatureSwitchPage`), with the one-time player (`player.tsx`: the question, the choices, the verdict with the working, Next, the result). The phone's practice screen, when its read answers 402, draws the same sets above the plan card and opens each in the player's `once` mode (`OnceQuiz` in `screens/practice/quiz.tsx`, no outbox: an answer offline is refused and tried again by hand). The teacher's assignment flow is unchanged.
- **Not built, as decided:** a second attempt or a retake the teacher grants; the one-time score on the guardian's practice page (the guardian's read is the child's plan's); a size other than ten.
