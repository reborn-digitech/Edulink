## Context

On 21 November 2026 the owner asked for the operator to switch premium features on for the students and teachers of chosen schools, to set a price with a discount for all schools or one school, and to choose a price, a free period and a renewal date when onboarding a school ("could be 50-200 rs/yr with 6 months free"). The owner then corrected the first draft: "There are two aspects to it: 1. School subscription: Schools operational features are enabled. 2. Add on premium subscription for teachers/students. Both are different". Three facts of the code shaped the answer: a school's subscription status is a record that switches nothing off (`operator-dashboard.ts:31-32`); a college is already priced a student a year by its tier, ₹199 or ₹299 (`packages/contracts/src/v1/operator-college.ts:25-28`); and every existing school was agreed on terms the app never held as a price a student a year. On the same day the owner answered eight choices in two dialogs that morning, approved the database change, and answered four more in a third dialog that afternoon (asked 14:22, answered 17:36 IST), four to a dialog, each with its own options (below, "The owner's choices"); one of the morning's answers, not the recommended option, makes a suspended school read-only for its people. That is part 4. A school keeps children's records, so part 4 says exactly what a paused school can still do and why.

## The decision

### Part 1. The school's subscription

1. **What it is.** What a school pays the company for everyday Shalaghar, for the whole school, priced **a student a year, in whole rupees, before GST**. **One price for everything** (taken): which optional parts a school has on never changes the price. Modules stay the school's to switch (and the operator's, for ASSISTANT).
2. **Our price and a school's own terms.** One price and one discount for all schools, starting at **₹100 a student a year with no discount** (taken). A school follows our price, or has its own discount on it, or its own price, never both. Our discount never stacks on an own price; an own discount replaces ours and is not added to it; an own 0 % is a real "no discount". What a school pays is rounded to the whole rupee, half up, in integer paise (₹199 at 50 % is ₹100; ₹199 at 10 % is ₹179). A change to our price changes at once the figure of every school that follows it or has its own discount on it; own-price schools do not move. A year already recorded as paid is never billed again (decision 14), so a paid-up school meets a new price in its next year's bill, and a school in its free months or with a year owed meets it in the bill it has not yet paid. A school's price is held, whatever ours later does, only where it has its own price, which the operator may give at onboarding or later.
3. **Colleges.** A college's price is its tier (₹199 affiliated, ₹299 autonomous), under TA-488's kind rule (`planSuitsKind`, `packages/contracts/src/v1/operator-college.ts:43-46`). A college may have its own discount on its tier, never its own price; our discount applies to the tiers. Once its terms are set, a college with no tier has no price, no bill and no partner figure until its tier is chosen ("No tier chosen"); while its terms are not set, a college on an ordinary plan keeps today's reading (decisions 6 and 7). Junior colleges are priced as schools (not in `COLLEGE_KINDS`, `packages/contracts/src/v1/modules.ts:60`).
4. **The Plan keeps its limits and stops being where a price is written.** No page writes a Plan's monthly price; the plans door ignores it for one release; its only read for money is decision 6's terms-not-set branch. So the old monthly price of an existing school's plan can no longer be changed: what such a school pays is changed by setting its terms. A plan made once this is built has no monthly price, so an ACTIVE school on such a plan whose terms are not set earns its partner nothing, which is today's reading of a plan with no price (a college tier is the exception: it is reckoned from its code and the roll, never from a monthly price, decision 6): the Partners page names it under its partner among the schools not counted for want of a price, and the dashboard counts it in the partners card's line for schools with no price (`unpricedSchools`, summed at `apps/web/src/app/root/page.tsx:55`). The remedy is to set the school's terms, after which decision 6's rule applies. That line says today that such schools are "on no plan" (`root.partnerUnpricedOne`, `root.partnerUnpricedMany`); it is reworded with this so that it stays true. (The author's choice: version 1 left the Plan's price untouched.) The limits are as they were: only the monthly text-message limit refuses anything; the others are flagged on the usage page (`apps/web/src/lib/plans.ts:26-31`).
5. **What the status does in parts 1 to 3.** A lapse, a passed renewal or DUE closes nothing. SUSPENDED and ENDED stop premium (decision 9) and refuse a payment (decision 15); neither ever touches a school's module switches. What SUSPENDED and ENDED do to a school's everyday work is part 4, accepted separately: accepting parts 1 to 3 accepts no lock, and until part 4 is accepted and built the two statuses close nothing else, as today. No status moves by itself on a date: a TRIAL school whose free months have ended stays TRIAL and reads "Free period ended"; an ACTIVE school whose renewal day has come stays ACTIVE and reads "Renewal passed"; DUE is set at onboarding or by the operator's correction (decisions 13 and 15).
6. **The partner's commission** (taken, Q5: "Paid-up subscription only"). For a school whose terms are set, a partner's month is their percent of the subscription month (what the school pays a student a year, after any discount, × its newest pupil count ÷ 12), every kind, while the school is ACTIVE and its renewal day has not yet come (on the renewal day itself nothing is owed: `renewalReached`); premium is never shared; a SUSPENDED or ENDED school earns nothing (it is not ACTIVE). ACTIVE is the status the operator records as paid up: Record payment sets it, and the corrections card and the old onboarding path can still set it with no payment recorded, and a partner earns on those too. A school in its free months (TRIAL), or with a year owed (DUE), earns its partner nothing, though it is using Shalaghar. The figure is this month's, worked out on read at today's price and newest count; no month is stored, so a change to our price moves a paid-up school's partner figure at once, though the school meets the new price only in its next bill. ₹0 is "free by agreement", not "unpriced", and earns nothing; of the schools whose terms are set, only one with no count, or a college with no tier, is unpriced. For a school whose terms are not set, today's reading (their percent of its plan's old monthly price, or for a college on a tier of a twelfth of the tier × the month's count, while ACTIVE, whatever the renewal date); setting a school's terms moves its partner from that reading to this one, which may be less. Two readings here are the author's, not the option's words: the option says "price", and the figure is what the school pays after any discount; the option says "not past its renewal date", and the build counts the renewal day itself as reached, since it is the day the next unpaid year starts.
7. **Terms not set** (taken for the schools that exist: "No change until I set terms"). Every school that exists when this is built starts with `School.termsSetAt` null. So does every school made afterwards on the plain New school page (`/root/new`) or by self sign-up, because only the onboarding step sets terms (the author's extension: the owner's answer spoke of existing schools). Until the operator sets its terms (one school, or "Put on our prices" for many) nothing about its price changes: no new price shown, no notice about a price, no bill, out of the Subscriptions page's totals, the partner's figure read as today. Premium may be switched on at such a school and covers its people (decision 9), and its office is told (decision 17), but the app shows no bill for it, premium included, until its terms are set. Having no terms set exempts a school from nothing in decision 10.

### Part 2. The premium add-on

8. **What it is.** The operator may put a school on premium for its students, its teachers, or both. Everyone on that roll in that role passes the plan lock for that plan's features with no plan of their own: Students the STUDENT plan's whole list, Teachers the TEACHER plan's whole list (`packages/contracts/src/v1/person-plans.ts:26-30`). It is a cover, one step in `planLockFor` (`apps/web/src/lib/person-plans.ts:161-167`) after the inactive-plan answer (:164) and before the signed-out answer (:165); it writes nothing per person. Cover follows the session's active school, a guardian's reading of a child the child's school. It switches on no module (0002, row 36): Vidura is such a module, the operator's to switch school by school (`apps/web/src/lib/modules.ts:143-152`), so a school on premium has Vidura only where Vidura is also switched on for it. Teachers premium is hidden at a college.
9. **Live cover** is: the switch on, the status not SUSPENDED or ENDED, the school not removed, whether or not its terms are set; worked out on read. That SUSPENDED and ENDED stop cover is carried over from version 1 by the author: no option the owner chose says it. **Nothing stops cover by itself** (taken, C: "Stays on until I act"): at a school that has not paid, premium runs on until the operator switches it off or the status becomes SUSPENDED or ENDED. The operators are reminded each week of every school due within seven days or past due (`remindSubscriptions`, `apps/web/src/lib/subscriptions.ts`).
10. **Three switches, in this order** (taken, A: "Yes, lock the rest"). (a) The platform's feature switch stays above everything (0006:17): a feature the operator has off is off for everyone, premium or not. (b) Each plan is "Asked for" or "Not asked for" on the Platform features page (`/root/features`), one switch for all schools. While a plan is not asked for it asks nothing (`apps/web/src/lib/person-plans.ts:52-56`, `:164`): everyone in that role uses its features wherever the feature is on, and premium adds nothing. (c) A school's premium switch. **Premium therefore makes a difference only once the plan is asked for; and from that moment that plan's features are shut, at once, for everyone in that role at every school without premium, the schools that exist today included and whether or not a school's terms are set, unless the person holds a plan of their own** (bought, or granted by the operator: a plan held opens every feature the plan lists while it runs, save one the operator has revoked). A person shut out keeps the menu entry and meets the plan card there (a door answers 402 `PLAN_REQUIRED`); a pupil still opens, once, a set a teacher assigned (0008:9); and a guardian then reads a child's practice only while the child holds the plan or is covered. The plan card always shows the plan, its price (₹99 a month or ₹999 a year as the plans stand) and Subscribe, which opens the Plans page. The same switch puts a person's own plan on sale there once it is priced and the company's gateway and invoice details are set (0006:58); until then the Plans page says "This plan cannot be bought here yet." to everyone, so a person shut out has the features only if the operator grants them a plan. Once a plan is on sale, a pupil under eighteen is told to ask a guardian (0006:59). Setting the plan back to "Not asked for" opens its features to everyone again and takes the plan off sale. Until the cover is built (phase 4), asking for a plan locks every school alike, the schools with premium included. On 21 November 2026 at 14:05 IST, on the development copy, no plan was asked for and of all these features only the Job board was switched on (read through the operator's doors): asking for the Teacher plan that day would at once have stopped every teacher who holds no plan of their own from reading the Job board. Answer A accepts the "Asked for" switch as the lever; the author reads that as confirming 0006:28's reading of "active" as "asked for".
11. **Its price**, starting at **₹50 more a student a year with no discount** (taken; the option the owner chose calls the ₹50 "only a placeholder"), with its own all-schools discount and a school's own discount or own price, in the order of decision 2, at every kind of school, never the tier: a college pays it on top of its tier. It is counted by students only (taken, B: "Same per-student price"): teachers are never counted or charged, so a school with Teachers premium only and 412 pupils pays 412 × ₹50, as it would for Students or for both, and adding Teachers to Students costs nothing more. **Premium is billed only while its plan is asked for** (taken, D: "Not billed until then"): the premium line is on a school's bill while, for at least one role, the school's switch is on and that role's plan is asked for; otherwise the bill leaves premium out, and the Subscriptions page says why (the build says it wherever the bill is shown). The rule reads the plan alone, as the owner's option does: not the platform's feature switches, and not the school's status. Onboarding starts a premium box ticked only when our offer's default for it is on and that role's plan is asked for; otherwise it starts unticked, and the operator may still tick it. Asking for a plan, or ceasing to, therefore moves the bills of the schools with that role's premium on, at that moment; their offices and heads are told as they are when our prices move their figure (the author's reading of decision 17, not the owner's words).
12. **A person who already holds a plan** (bought, or granted by the operator) keeps it to its end, with no refund or proration because of the cover (0006:63); the operator may still refund a plan in full within seven days of payment, as before (0006:62). While covered they are offered no Pay (INCLUDED; a new key, `passes.refuseIncluded`).

### Part 3. Both parts: the year, the bill and the payment

13. **One renewal date per school** (`subscriptionDueAt`) covers both parts and means **the day the next unpaid year starts**. Free months are offered in the onboarding form only (0, 1, 3, **6 by default, taken**, or 12 calendar months) and cover both parts: TRIAL, renewal = start + months. A typed renewal date is the first day after the free period, its months said back; over twelve months asks a tick; a renewal on or before today, typed or reached by the free months, asks a tick too when the school's start day is before today (a school that starts today with no free months needs none). No free months (the renewal is the start day, even a start still ahead), or a date on or before today: DUE at once. In these rules a day is the school's own, in its time zone (the brief's clarifications C1b and C1c). Old callers keep today's rule (`subscriptions.ts:624-628`).
14. **The bill** is worked out on read and stored nowhere: newest pupil count × (subscription pays + premium pays while premium is billed, decision 11), each rounded first, at today's prices, and every place that shows it says so. There is no bill without terms set, a renewal date, a pupil count and, at a college, a tier. No part years: premium that begins to be billed after a year is paid costs nothing until the next renewal, and pupils who join are not billed until then. A part that comes to ₹0 (at 100 %, or by rounding) is no line on the bill and reads "Free by agreement". Because nothing is stored, the figure can move between the company's hand-made invoice and the payment (a new count, a new price, a plan asked for); a payment unlike the bill is recorded only with a tick (decision 15).
15. **Recording a payment** pays the next unpaid year of both parts: the renewal moves twelve months on from the renewal on record, whether the school pays early or late, in its free period or after, and the status becomes ACTIVE; with no status or no renewal on record the year starts on the day paid. **Refused while SUSPENDED or ENDED**: the status is changed first (on the corrections card today; by Resume once part 4 is built), and a payment never changes those statuses by itself. Compare-and-set in one `$tenantTransaction` with its audit row; an amount unlike the bill only with a tick. **Correcting the dates of a school never recorded as paid:** the dates call for DUE when the renewal is the start day or is on or before today, and for TRIAL otherwise; while nothing is recorded as paid, before and after the write, a move between TRIAL and DUE is allowed only to the status the dates, as they will stand after the write, call for, in one audited write; any other move the status list refuses stays refused, and a date changed alone moves no status (the brief's clarifications C1 to C1c). The corrections card may move an unpaid school's renewal at any time: that is how a free period is lengthened or cut short after onboarding. For this `setSubscription` accepts a renewal on the start day.
16. **Nothing is collected in the app.** The school pays on the company's invoice, outside the app; the company's own revenue (0006:53), never a school's fee money (0003:7).
17. **Audit and who is told.** The platform's log holds every write (`superuser.school.premium`, `superuser.school.terms`, `operator.offer.update`, the onboarding row, `superuser.school.subscription`). The school's own log, which the school reads in its safety centre, holds a terms save (the parts that changed, that its terms were first set, and the reason if the operator typed one), a premium switch (and its reason, if typed), a payment (the status and the renewal only) and a change of status, start or renewal on the corrections card, with its reason; it never holds the operator's note, a payment's amount, a reference, an invoice or payment date, or the partner (a terms save does show the school its own price or discount as it changed). A change to the note alone, a change to our offer and onboarding are in the platform's log only. The office and head are told in the app when premium goes on or off, when terms are set or changed, and when our prices move their figure; a school whose terms are not set is never told about a price. Students and teachers are not told.
18. **Counts without looking inside.** Every count comes from `UsageSample`, the row a nightly job already writes of each school's pupils on the roll (`apps/web/src/lib/plans.ts:17-21`; readable by the operator and never writable from a page, `apps/web/prisma/rls.sql:1680-1684`); no page built here counts members, so these pages write no visit row (0009:16). The colleges page's own count of students enrolled, which it takes today from the memberships (`apps/web/src/lib/operator-college.ts:151`), is not this record's and is not changed: a college whose terms are not set keeps today's line on that page, and once its terms are set its year is reckoned on `UsageSample` like every other figure here.

### Part 4. A paused school is read-only

#### The rule

19. **While a school's recording is closed its people can read, print and download everything they could before, and record nothing but the writes this part keeps open.** Recording is closed while `School.recordingClosedAt` is set, which only the operator's Pause (status SUSPENDED) or End (ENDED; taken, Q2) sets, and only Resume or Restore clears. Nothing closes or opens by a date; no job sets or clears it. A column and not the status alone, so a school already SUSPENDED before this is built does not lock on the day it ships with nobody told (choice Q4), and the school can be told "since when". The option the owner chose reads "The school's people can still read but cannot record anything until you make it Active again"; the exceptions below were taken as a whole (Q1: "The safety list") and are the owner's to confirm line by line when part 4 is accepted.
20. **"Read" includes** every GET and HEAD, a read that writes a side row (who opened a health card), exports and prints, and POSTs that only read, each declared: the five-minute file link by which the phone opens every PDF, and the server actions that only load or search. The list of read-only POSTs and actions is complete before the lock is pushed.
21. **One check, default-deny.** The session's gate (`gateRequest`, `apps/web/src/lib/role-gate.ts:230`), which every handler and session-checking action reaches through `getSession`, refuses a write at a closed school unless the API catalog entry carries `openWhilePaused` with its reason, or the page is listed as open. It runs after the existing answers (a feature that is off, another kind of institution, a role the door does not serve answer as they do today), so a refusal never reveals a door. A door added later is closed until someone declares it. **The operator's own doors and pages are never judged**, whatever school the operator's session carries, so the operator can always resume. A guardian is refused when at least one child and every child's school is closed; with children at an open and a closed school, the refusal is per child, and a family write that names no child is refused while any child's school is closed. Writes into a paused school from outside its session are judged in the handler: joining by code, accepting an invitation, linking a child, an admission application and a recruiter's "joined" are refused; a public survey answer and a website enquiry are received; an expert's own work is open. Uploads stay open (a file is named by a later write, which is judged).
22. **The refusal** is its own error, a kind of access refusal that is not "page not found": a door answers **423** with the code `RECORDING_PAUSED` (or `RECORDING_ENDED`), a form shows the sentence, and the register's door answers 423 too, not 500. The phone's outbox and the web's queue keep such a write for thirty days whichever school is active, show it waiting, send it when the school is open again, and say "not saved" if it is finally dropped; register marks made before the pause are accepted. The lock is not switched on while a phone build that would drop a 423 may be in use (the server answers such builds 503, or none is shown installed).

#### What stays open, and why

23. Each line is declared in the catalog with its reason. Taken as a whole (Q1: "The safety list"). The dialog named the groups in short (below, "The owner's choices"); the lines inside each group are this record's reading of them. **Not named in the dialog's words, and so the first lines to confirm when part 4 is accepted:** joining another school, a guardian ending their own link and a pupil's own practice (own account); the safety centre's take-down (harm); lectures, the absence alert, hostel leave decided, the hostel gate and visitors (where a child is); for a leaver, the lines beyond leaving itself (certificates revoked, asked and declined, the record's correction, a record-change decision, the signature, a college's no-dues); and what 0006:30 keeps open for sessions (money and sessions in motion).
    - **A person's own account**, door by door: signing in, two-step, choosing a school, devices, notifications, web push, the account pages, joining another school, a guardian ending their own link, a pupil's own practice. *Without them a person cannot sign in, keep the account safe, leave a link or learn.*
    - **Consent given or taken back**: a college student's grants, permission slips, mentoring, hostel leave asks and consent, competition entries, session seats, a minor's placement, the assistant opt-out, a record-change ask withdrawn. *Consent belongs to the person; taking it back must never be harder than giving it.*
    - **Reports of harm**: the safety centre's take-down, the incident log (school, hostel, campus squad), a college student's grievance or ragging complaint, its escalation and acknowledgement. *A report that a child is harmed must be receivable whatever the subscription; some carry a legal clock.*
    - **Where a child is**: the day's register and lectures, the absence alert, a family's absence note, the bus's trips (start, positions, boardings, end), the hostel's roll call, leave decided, gate out and in, visitors. *A legal register and a child's whereabouts; a bus gone dark leaves no record of who got off.*
    - **The health and emergency card**, by the family and by the office or head (check-ups closed). *Staff must see a new allergy, and a family without the app phones the office.*
    - **A pupil leaving, with correct papers**: leaving, certificates issued, revoked, asked and declined, and for a leaver the record's correction, a record-change decision, the certificate's signature and a college's leaving no-dues. Not the certificate fee. *An unpaid invoice must not hold a child; the family's correction right stays with the school (0002:8).*
    - **Cutting someone's access**: a guardian's link ended for a coded reason such as a court order, a person paused or restored, staff leaving with a past day, an attendance device disabled or rekeyed. *Access the school must withdraw for a child's safety cannot wait.*
    - **Money and sessions in motion**: a family paying the school's fees online (the school's own gateway, 0003; the webhook records it); what 0006:30 keeps open for a lapsed plan (payee details, a provider cancelling what they offered, a booking cancelled or reported not held, a paid seat confirmed). *Money has moved or is the school's own; 0006:30 stays true.*
    - **One notice a day to the school's families**, by the office, audited. *A school shut by rain must be able to say so; families are not told of the pause.*
24. **Everything else closes**: marks, report cards, homework, timetables, other notices, messages between people, counter receipts, admissions, library, transport and hostel set-up, payroll, staff self check-in (the school's staff record, not a child's; punch machines still record), leave, record and PEN changes but a leaver's, signatures but certificates, the pass sale and "ask my guardians", the assistant. Doors with no session keep working: the school's gateway webhook, message delivery reports, punch machines. Jobs carry out what was recorded before the pause and skip what asks people to do what the school cannot record (fee and library reminders, offer expiry, new social posts); the website's enquiries are not deleted for want of an answer while paused; retention runs as at any school.

#### What people see

25. **Nothing is hidden or greyed**; a refused write says why, in the reader's language, in one of three forms. **The office and the head**: "Recording is paused at {school} since {date}. You can still read, print and download everything. To make it active again, contact Shalaghar: {contact}.", also as a banner on every page of theirs. **Other staff**: the same without the contact, "For questions, please ask the school office." **Pupils and families**: "This can't be saved in Shalaghar just now. You can still read and download everything, and nothing already saved is lost. Please ask the school office." — no date, no "paused", no amount: a family or a child is never told the school's standing, and no plan card invites a child to pay because the school has not ("Not available at your school just now."). The register and marks screens show the sentence before anyone starts. A family's fee page says payments made at the office since the pause may not show yet. The operator's reason is never in the banner; the head reads it in the safety centre. Ended uses "ended" words in the same forms.

#### What the operator does

26. **Pause, resume, warn, end and restore are deliberate acts**, one school at a time, on the web only, through `POST /api/v1/operator/schools/{schoolId}/standing` and `school-standing.ts`, each with a preview, a typed reason and a compare-and-set, in one transaction with its audit rows. **Warn** (taken, Q3: "Required, 7 days"): the office and the head are told in the app, with the reason, that recording may be paused from the seventh day after the warning; Pause is refused before that day, and nothing pauses by itself. As built in phase 1 the act names no later day: the ten approved columns keep only when the warning was given (`School.recordingWarnedAt`), so the earliest day is always the warning's day plus seven (`earliestPauseDay`).
