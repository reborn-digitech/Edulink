Roles: PARENT

# The guardian's guide

This guide is for a parent or guardian of a pupil at a school on Shalaghar, and, in its last part, of a student at a college. You start at `/parent`, the "Overview". The bottom bar on a phone and the rail on a desk carry "Overview", "Attendance", "Assignments", "Exams" and "Messages". The "More" menu groups the rest: "Day to day" holds "Certificates", "Practice", "Timetable", "Meetings", "Fees", "Health card", "Incidents", "Bus", "Feedback", "Requests to link" and "Assistant"; "School life" holds "Social Media", "Explore schools", "Applications", "Enquiries", "Competitions", "Mentoring", "Sessions", "Past years", "Library" and "Hostel"; "Running things" holds "Settings". "Bus", "Library", "Hostel" and "Assistant" appear only while the child's school has that module on; a college child adds "College attendance", "College results", "Placement", "My money" and "Surveys". The command palette offers "Message a teacher", "Notification settings" and "Exam marks".

## How do I switch between my children, or link another?

Every page about one child carries a row of chips, "Choose a child", when you have more than one; the chip keeps you on the page you are on, and the child is in the address so you can bookmark it. To link a child, press "Link another child" at the foot of the "Overview" (`/parent/link`): enter the "Student code" (on the letter from school, or in the child's app under "Me"), "Their date of birth" and "You are their…" ("Parent", "Guardian" or "Other family member"), then press "Link to my child". A code alone is never enough; the school's office, or a guardian already linked, confirms a request that does not match, and you are told.

## What does the Overview show?

Open `/parent`. The page names the child, their school and grade. Cards at the top ask for what is waiting on you: "Complete the school record" when the record lacks a detail a certificate needs, "Permission slips to answer", a PEN correction that needs your approval, and "Waiting for you" for a competition entry ("They cannot take part until you say yes."). "Today" says whether the child was marked present, late or absent, with any note the teacher wrote. Tiles show "Attendance" over four weeks, "Average grade" across marked homework and "Attendance streak". Then "{name}’s lessons today", "Coming up" (work due soon, with "What is coming up" for the fortnight's agenda at `/parent/agenda`), "Teacher messages" and the latest "Exam marks".

## How do I read my child's attendance and tell the school about a day away?

Open `/parent/attendance` ("Attendance"). The month is in the title; "Previous month", "Next month" and "This month" move it. Tiles show "Overall" ("{there} of {total} days"), "Late", "Absent" and "Excused"; "Day by day" lists each recorded day with its mark and the teacher's note. Anything a teacher changes appears here immediately. Below 90% the page suggests a word with the teacher on the messages page.

1. Under "Absence notes", set "Day away" (today or up to sixty days ahead) and the "Reason" ("Illness", "Family matter", "Religious observance", "Travel" or "Other").
2. Add "A line for the teacher (optional)" and press "Tell the school".

The teacher sees "Absent — {reason}, told by the family" on the register for that day, and you are not sent an absence notice for it. "Take it back" withdraws a note until the register has marked the day.

## How do I see the timetable and the homework?

`/parent/timetable` ("Timetable") shows the child's real week with the teacher of each lesson; "← Previous", "This week" and "Next →" move it, and whoever is covering an absent teacher shows in their place. `/parent/assignments` ("Assignments") has the tabs "Due", "Handed in" and "Marked": each piece with its class and category, "due {when}", and "Handed in", "Overdue" or "Not handed in yet"; the "Marked" tab shows the mark out of the points and the teacher's comment. It is read-only: homework is between the child and the teacher.

## How do I see exam marks and report cards?

Open `/exams` ("Exams", or the quick action "Exam marks"). "Marks" shows each child's papers as soon as a teacher saves them, filtered by "Unit test", "Mid-term" or "Final exam"; "Upcoming" shows the published date sheet; "Report cards" lists each child's published cards with the date and overall percentage, and opening one shows the card as printed. Cards are prepared once the school marks the term finished.

## How do I write to a teacher, and answer one?

Open `/remarks` ("Messages"). "About a pupil" lists each conversation about your child with their teacher; a dot marks unread. Press "New message" to write to a person, or use the quick action "Message a teacher" (`/remarks/new`): choose the child, give a "Subject", write the "Message" and press "Send". The teacher, your child and the child's other guardians read the same thread. Reply at the foot of a thread.

## How do I book a meeting with a teacher?

Open `/parent/meetings` ("Meetings"). "Open times" lists slots from teachers who actually teach your children, with the time, place and note; press "Book" (choose the child when the teacher teaches more than one). The teacher is told ("Booked. The teacher has been told."). "Your meetings" lists your bookings; "Cancel" frees the slot for another family.

## How do I see and pay the fees?

Open `/parent/fees` ("Fees"). The tiles show "Outstanding", "Overdue" and "Paid this year". "Instalments" lists each with "Due on", "Gross", "Concession", "Net", "Paid", "Outstanding" and "Status"; "Payment calendar" lists every due date and the reminders you will get. If the office has opened plans to families and the child is not yet on one, "Choose a fee plan" lists them with their instalments: press "Choose this plan", then "Confirm".

1. Under "Choose what to pay", tick the instalments ("Everything outstanding" ticks all) and read the "Total".
2. Press "Pay online" to pay at the school's own gateway in its checkout; the page then says "Paid. Receipt {serial}." with "Receipt".
3. Or press "Pay by UPI": press "Open UPI app" (the school is the payee and the amount is filled in) or "Copy UPI id", pay, then enter the "UTR from your UPI app" and press "I have paid — send the UTR". The office confirms it against the bank statement, and the payment shows "Waiting for the office to confirm" until your receipt appears.
4. Where the school gives a "Bank transfer" note, transfer to the account shown and bring the reference to the office.

You are paying the school; Shalaghar does not receive or hold the payment. "Payments" lists every payment with its method, state and "Receipt {serial}". If the page says "This school has not set up a way to pay from here yet. Pay at the office.", pay at the counter.

## How do I ask for a certificate for my child?

Open `/parent/certificates` ("Certificates"). "Certificates" lists every child's issued papers with "Open" and "Download PDF". Pick the child, then under "Ask for a certificate" choose the "Certificate" (the kinds the school has a format for, such as "Bonafide certificate" or "Transfer certificate") and "What it is for", and press "Send the request". "Your requests" shows each as "Waiting for the office", "Issued", "Declined" or "Taken back"; a fee the office sets reads "Fee {amount}: pay it on the Fees page, or at the office." and "Take it back" withdraws a waiting ask. The office issues from the child's pupil record.

## How do I write my child's health card?

Open `/parent/health` ("Health card"). It is what a teacher must know before the ambulance question is asked, read by the child's teachers.

1. Press "Edit the card".
2. Set the "Blood group", "Allergies" (and what to do), "Conditions", "Medicines" (what, when, where kept), "Diet", "Teachers should know", the "Family doctor" and up to three people under "Who to call" ("Add a person": "Name", "Relation", "Phone").
3. Press "Save the card".

"Check-ups" lists the school's own measurements (height, weight, sight); the family reads them and never edits them. "Print the card" opens a printable copy. "Who has read this" lists the members of staff who opened the card in the last ninety days.

## How do I read an incident the school recorded?

Open `/parent/incidents` ("Incidents"). Each incident or sick-room visit the school recorded about the child is listed newest first, as the school wrote it: what happened, what was done, how serious, and the "Follow-up" notes. The school tells you at once when it records one, and the notification opens that record. Only the school writes here.

## How do I correct the pupil record, and approve a PEN change?

Open `/parent/record` ("Open the record" on the Overview's card, or from "Certificates"). The record is what the school issues the child's certificates from. "Still blank: {fields}" names what is missing, and "Identity card (PDF)" downloads the card.

1. Press "Ask for a change".
2. Change what is wrong or fill in what is blank; the PEN and the roll number are the office's.
3. Write "Why this should change" and press "Send to the office".

The record changes only when the office approves; "Waiting for the office" shows the ask and "Take it back" withdraws it. When the office proposes a correction to the child's PEN, the Overview shows "A correction to {name}’s PEN needs your approval"; open the record to approve or reject it, since the principal and a guardian must both agree.

## How do I see my child's practice?

Open `/parent/practice` ("Practice"). It lists every chapter the child has worked on, subject by subject, with the "Level" they stand on, how many questions they "Tried" and the "Badges" earned out of 6, and the questions in the last seven days. Practice is answered on the child's own sign-in; this page compares the child only with the ladder, never with anybody else's child.

## How do I follow the school bus?

Open `/parent/transport` ("Bus"). Only when the school has the Transport module on. The card shows "Bus {bus}", the route and the stop, "Picked up at {time}" and "Set down at {time}", and "Driver: {name}" with a phone number to call. Tick "Tell me when the bus is near" for a notice as the bus approaches the child's stop. During a trip, "The bus is on its way" shows the bus on the map, "Expected at {stop} between {from} and {to}", the stops passed, and when the child boarded or alighted; "Last seen {minutes} minutes ago" warns of a stale feed. The page shows the bus, never the child's own location. "No trip today" means none runs the way the child rides.

## How do I answer a permission slip, acknowledge a circular, or consent to a competition?

"Permission slips to answer" on the Overview lists each question with "For {names}" and "Answer by {date}"; press "Answer" to open the notice on `/bulletin` and press "Yes" or "No" for each child, changeable until the day. A circular that asks for it has a "Seen and understood" button. When a child asks to enter a competition, "Waiting for you" on the Overview and `/competitions` show "{child} would like to enter {title}, run by {organiser}." with "Allow" or "Not this time"; the child cannot take part until you say yes.

## How do I decide who else may link to my child?

Open `/parent/claims` ("Requests to link"; it appears in the menu only while somebody waits). Somebody typed your child's student code and date of birth; approve only if you know them ("Approve" or "Decline" with a sentence for them). The school's office can decide too.

## Where are the library and the hostel?

`/parent/library` ("Library"; with the Library module) shows the child's "On loan", "Reservations", "Fines" (a fine on the fee account is paid with the fees; any other at the school office; Shalaghar never takes the money) and "The catalogue", with "Reserve" for the child. `/parent/hostel` ("Hostel"; with the Hostel module) shows the child's room and bed, "Tonight" (the roll call), "Today's menu" and "Leave": ask for leave yourself under "Ask for leave" ("Your ask is your consent: it goes straight to the warden."), or answer the child's own ask with "Consent" or "Do not consent"; "Take the ask back" withdraws one before the child goes out.

## How do I find a school, enquire and apply?

Open `/explore` ("Explore schools"): search by name, narrow by country and state, and open a school's page. Under "Apply for a place" (when a cycle is open) read the grades, "{remaining} of {seats} seats left", the documents asked for and whether there is an entrance test, then press "Apply": give the "Child's name", "Date of birth", "Grade applied for", "Relation to the child", "Last school", "City", the "UDISE+ PEN (if the child has one)", "Home address" and "Anything else", and press "Send the application". Under the admissions heading, write an enquiry and press "Send enquiry"; the school's reply lands in `/enquiries` ("Enquiries"). `/parent/applications` ("Applications") lists every application with its stage and "Next step"; open one to "Attach" the documents, "Book this sitting" for an entrance test, answer an offer with "Accept the seat" or "Decline", or "Withdraw the application". Once admitted, the child's school pages are yours.

## How do I agree to mentoring, book a session, or rate a teacher?

`/mentoring` ("Mentoring"): when the school pairs your child with a mentor, the page asks "Do you agree to this mentoring going ahead?" with "Yes, go ahead" or "No, end it"; nobody can send a message until you agree, and you can read every message and "End this mentorship" at any time. `/sessions` ("Sessions") shows each child's free allowance, "Your sessions" and "Open to book": press "Book this" and say "What do you want from it?"; a child's own ask waits for your "Agree" or "Decline". A paid workshop seat (never a one-to-one) shows "Pay {amount}". After a session, "Rate it" out of five. `/reviews` ("Feedback") lists the open review rounds for the teachers of your children: tap stars for each question and press "Send"; your name is never shown.

## What are Social Media and Past years?

`/bulletin` ("Social Media") is the school's feed: notices, celebrations and reminders, with a chip per school when your children are at two. A guardian reads and reacts; posting is for pupils and staff. `/archive` ("Past years") shows a closed year for one child: attendance, marks, report cards and badges, exactly as it was when the year closed.

## How do I set my notifications, the assistant, and my account?

Open `/parent/settings` ("Settings", or the quick action "Notification settings"). "Notifications" sets the channels you are reached on and the quiet hours; these settings apply to every child above. "Who you hear about" lists each linked child with "Linked" and "Remove" (type the child's name to confirm); "Link another" opens the link page. "The assistant" shows a box per child, "{name} may use the assistant", ticked while they may; untick it to turn the assistant off for that child. "Download my data" gives "Download for {name}" per child. "Your account" opens `/account` for appearance, signed-in devices and signing out.

## What does the assistant answer for me?

Press "Ask the assistant" on any page or open `/assistant` ("Assistant"). For a guardian it answers questions about using Shalaghar: which page to open and the steps to take there, and it can read your child's timetable, exams, assignments, attendance and fees to answer "how much fee is due" or "when is the next exam". Questions about topics are for students and teachers; it is not a general chatbot. Type in "Your question" and press "Ask"; do not type names or phone numbers. Each conversation is deleted thirty days after it was last used.

## At a college

A guardian of a college student sees four more pages. For a student under eighteen they always open; for an adult student they open only for what the student has granted on their "What my family sees" page, and otherwise say "Not shared with you".

- `/parent/academics` ("College attendance"): lecture attendance per course, as the college records it.
- `/parent/results` ("College results"): the exam form, "Hall ticket and seats", "Where you stand", each published result, "Grade cards and transcripts", "Backlogs" and "Credit statement", read-only.
- `/parent/placement` ("Placement"): "Waiting for your consent" lists a minor's drive applications ("{drive} · {company}: the record goes to the company only once you consent.") with "I consent"; then "{name}'s offers" and "{name}'s internships".
- `/parent/money` ("My money"): the year's ledger by term, "Demand", "Scholarship covers", "Paid" and "You owe"; pay through the college's own gateway or UPI on the fees page, or at its counter.
- `/parent/voice` ("Surveys"): the surveys the child's college asks guardians to answer, once and without a name.
