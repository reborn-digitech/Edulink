Roles: FACULTY

# The faculty member's guide (college)

This guide is for a faculty member at a college: a teacher whose home is `/faculty`. It covers your day (lectures and their registers, homework, the course file, internal marks), the term (countersigning, mentoring, your exam duties, the internships and projects you supervise), the year (your research register, your public page, the surveys the IQAC opens to you), your own leave, attendance and letters, and the duties the office may give you beside your teaching. The menu has three groups: "Day to day" holds "Messages", "Exam duties", "Students' work", "Homework", "Practical record", "Moot courts" and "Assistant"; "School life" holds "E-resources"; "Running things" holds "Account", "Leave", "Staff attendance", "My letters", "My research", "My public page", "My appraisal" and the desk of each duty you hold. A page behind a module is only in the menu while the college has that module on; academics is always on at a college. A head of department reads this guide too: an HOD's teaching uses the same pages, under "My teaching" in their menu.

## How do I see my day?

Open `/faculty`. The page is headed "Good to see you, {your name}" with your department and the term under it.

1. Read the four tiles: "Lectures today", "Courses this term", "Weekly load" (your hours a week against the college's band, marked Under, Within or Over) and "To countersign".
2. Under "Lectures on {date}" each lecture of the day shows its time, course code, kind, division and room, with "Standing in" when you cover a colleague; press "Mark the lecture", or "Review the marks" once it is marked.
3. Under "My courses this term" each course you teach shows "Registered", "Coverage" (weeks taught of weeks planned, with "Behind" when the plan is two or more weeks behind the term), "Internal marks" (the parts, how many are countersigned and released, or "Locked") and "Below the rule".
4. Under "Countersign a colleague's marks" are the internal marks in your department waiting for your second signature.
5. The buttons at the foot open "Mentoring" and "My payslips".

If the office has given you a duty, a "My duties" card sits at the top with one card per duty and an "Open" link to its desk. On a holiday the page says "The college is closed today".

## How do I mark a lecture's attendance?

Open `/faculty`, find the lecture under "Lectures on {date}" and press "Mark the lecture". The register page is headed "Register: {n} students" and says "Present unless marked otherwise; late counts as attended. A student's percentage is against {threshold}%."

1. Every registered student is listed with their roll number and running percentage, green at or above the programme's rule and red below it.
2. Each student is "Present" unless you choose "Absent" or "Late".
3. Press "Save". When you come back to a marked lecture the button reads "Save changes".

The phone marks the same register. A second press of the same form writes nothing twice. A lecture whose attendance the exam cell has already used to decide eligibility cannot be changed: the page says "The exam eligibility was decided from this lecture's attendance, so it can no longer be changed. Lectures after the decision can still be marked." A lecture that is not yet markable says "This lecture cannot be marked now."

## How do I see a course's attendance by student?

Open `/faculty`, press the course's code under "My courses this term", and choose the "Attendance" tab. The table lists each registered student with "Attended" (lectures attended of lectures held), "Percent", "Standing" against the programme's rule and "Last notice" (when a defaulter notice last went to them). The HOD's defaulters page sends those notices; you only read them here. A student who is short asks for condonation from their own page, and the HOD or the principal decides it; a condoned shortfall reads "Condoned" and counts as eligible. The same table on your home's "Below the rule" column is this list's count per course.

## How do I write the teaching plan and keep the course file?

Open `/faculty`, press the course's code and stay on the "Course file" tab. The "Teaching plan" card tells you how far you are: "{taught} of {planned} weeks taught; {expected} expected by now, the term {elapsed}% through."

1. In "The plan", write one week a line as the hint says: "week | topic | outcomes | the day taught, when it was (1 | Units and measurement | CO1 | 2026-07-20)". The outcome codes are the course's own, listed above the plan.
2. Press "Save the plan". The whole plan is replaced by what you typed, so keep the earlier weeks in it.
3. Under "Resources and papers", choose "What it is" ("Resource", "Lecture notes", "Question paper", "Model answer" or "Link"), give a "Title", the "Outcomes" it serves (for example CO1 CO3), and either a "Link" or a "File"; press "Add". Students see the resources, never the papers.

The "Outcome attainment" card reads the internal marks: the share of students reaching the target on the parts that test each outcome, with the exit survey's part beside it once the IQAC has run one. The last card shows "Not reviewed by the HOD yet." until your head marks the file reviewed with a note.

## How do I set up the internal assessment and enter marks?

Open the course from `/faculty` and choose the "Internal marks" tab. The card is headed "Internal assessment out of {max}" and says "The parts' weights add to {total} of {max}. A colleague countersigns each part, the HOD releases it, and locks the marks for the exam cell."

1. Add each part with "Name", "Kind" ("Unit test", "Assignment", "Practical", "Viva" or "Project"), "Out of", "Weight" and the "Outcomes" it tests, then press "Add part". The weights may not pass the scheme's internal total.
2. To enter marks, choose the "Part", type each student's marks in the box beside their name (AB for absent), or paste a sheet in "Or paste a sheet": one student a line, roll number or student code, then the marks. Press "Save".
3. Each part shows "{n} of {of} entered" and "Not countersigned" until a colleague countersigns it.

A change to a part's marks takes its countersign away, so the colleague signs again. Once the HOD has released a part to the students it can no longer be entered, and once the HOD has locked the course the page says "Locked by {name}: no change is possible." The locked totals are what the exam cell takes into the results.

## How do I countersign a colleague's marks?

Open `/faculty`. The card "Countersign a colleague's marks" lists the parts in your department waiting for a second signature, each as "{course} · {part}" with the colleague's name; the "To countersign" tile counts them. Press one to open the course's "Internal marks" tab.

1. Read the marks in the table.
2. Press "Countersign" beside the part.

The part then reads "Countersigned by {your name}" and the HOD may release it. You can countersign only a part of your own department that you neither teach nor entered marks on; your own courses are signed by a colleague. A part that is changed after you signed loses the signature and comes back to the list.

## How do I set homework for a course and mark it?

Open `/faculty/work` ("Homework"). "Your courses" lists each course you teach with its term, division (or "Elective, shared across divisions"), "{count} registered", "Work set: {count}" and "To mark: {count}". Press a course.

1. Under "Set a piece of work", give the "Title", the "Instructions", the "Kind of work" (for example assignment, tutorial sheet, lab report), "Marks out of" (leave it blank for work that is not marked), "Due" and a "Handout".
2. Press "Publish" to send it to every student registered on the course, or "Save as a draft" to keep it to yourself. A due date already past is refused unless you tick "The due date has passed, and I mean that date."
3. "Work on this course" lists each piece with "Handed in: {done} of {of}" and "Marked: {done} of {of}"; press one to mark it.

On the work's page a draft shows "This work is a draft. Students cannot see it yet." with a "Publish" button. The marking table below is the school's marking screen: each registered student, what they handed in with its photographs, and a box for marks and a comment. A student's first mark tells them. Published work shows in each registered student's Assignments, and the guardians an adult student lets see results are told too.

## How do I post homework marks into the internal marks?

Open the piece of work from `/faculty/work`. The card "Post into internal marks" says "Copies each marked student's score into one internal component, scaled to its maximum. A change takes away the component's countersign."

1. Choose the "Component" (the part, shown as "{name} (out of {max})").
2. Press "Post the marks".

The work must have a maximum mark ("Give this work a maximum mark before posting it into internal marks."), the course must have an open part ("This course has no internal component open for marks. Add one on the course's internal marks tab."), and at least one registered student must be marked. Once the HOD has locked the course the card says "This course's internal marks are locked, so nothing more can be posted."

## How do I control what the assistant tells students about an assignment?

Open the piece of work from `/faculty/work`. The card "The assistant and this assignment" explains: "When a pupil asks the assistant a question that matches this assignment, it gives a hint first: the idea, the chapter and a guiding question, never the answer."

1. Tick or untick "Give the worked answer when the same question is asked a second time".
2. The change saves at once and the page says "The assistant's rule for this assignment is saved."

Ticked, a student who asks the same question again in the same conversation gets the worked answer; unticked, it stays a hint.

## How do I use the assistant?

Press the assistant's launcher in the top bar, or open `/assistant`. As a faculty member you have both halves: questions about using the platform (which page, which steps) and questions about topics in the books, answered with the book, chapter and page cited. It also answers questions about your own records from the pages you already see (today's lectures, students below the attendance threshold, components to moderate, your leave), counting from the platform's own figures, and under "Sources" it links the post or the message thread it read.

1. Press "New conversation", type in "Your question" and press "Ask". Do not type names, phone numbers or anyone's details.
2. "Your conversations" lists the last thirty days; a conversation is deleted thirty days after it was last used, or sooner with "Delete this conversation".
3. Under each answer, "Was this helpful?" takes a "Yes" or "No".

The assistant remembers only the conversation it is in. It is not a general chatbot: anything outside the platform and the books gets one sentence saying what it is for.

## How do I keep the practical record?

Only at a college with a stream that keeps labs (science, engineering, pharmacy, health sciences, agriculture or vocational). Open `/faculty/practicals` ("Practical record"). "Courses with practicals" lists each course you teach that is offered with practical hours or practical credits; press one.

1. The table shows each registered student with their "Batch" and a column per experiment ("Exp. 1", "Exp. 2" ...), a tick or the marks in each, and a seal where a row is signed.
2. Under "Record an experiment", give the "Experiment number" (the next one is filled in), the "Experiment", "Performed on" (today or earlier) and, under "Students and marks", untick anyone who did not perform it and type "Marks" (0 to 100, or blank for none). Press "Record the experiment".
3. Under "To sign", tick the rows and press "Sign the ticked rows".

Recording the same experiment number again corrects it until it is signed; a signed row is final. The course's teacher writes; the teacher or a lab in-charge signs. A course with no practicals does not appear here.

## How do I record a moot court?

Only at a law college; elsewhere the page says "Moot courts are kept by a college with the law stream; yours has none." Open `/faculty/moot-courts`.

1. "Moots" lists every moot the college held or sent teams to, with each team's outcome, the result and the judges' notes.
2. Under "Record a moot court", give the "Name of the moot", "Held on" and the "Kind" ("Intra-college", "Inter-college" or "National").
3. For "Team 1" and "Team 2" (and up to "Team 4") give the "Team name", the "Outcome" ("Winner", "Runner-up", "Best memorial", "Best speaker" or "Took part") and open "Choose the team's students" to tick them.
4. Add the "Result" and "Judges' notes" and press "Record the moot court".

Every team member must be a student of the college. A recorded moot is corrected by the person who recorded it, the office or the head of the law department.

## How do I see my exam duties and set a paper?

Only when the college has the Exams module on. Open `/faculty/exams` ("Exam duties"): "Halls to watch, papers to set, bundles to assess, and what the exam cell records as owed to you."

1. "Invigilation" lists each duty with its time, course, hall and session, marked "Assigned", "Swapped" or "Done". The exam cell assigns, swaps and marks them.
2. "Papers to set" appears at an autonomous college when the exam cell has appointed you a setter: upload "The paper (PDF)", type the "Outcome map: one line a question" (for example "Q1 CO1 10") and press "Seal and send". The paper is sealed the moment you send it; you may replace it until it is marked "Moderated".
3. "Central assessment" lists the bundles of answer books allotted to you, each "with you" or "Returned".
4. "Remuneration" is "A record of what is owed; the college pays it outside the app."

Until you have a duty the page says "No exam duties for you yet." If the office has made you a member of the exam flying squad you also have the "Exam flying squad" desk; see the duties below.

## How do I supervise an internship or guide a project?

Only when the college has the Placement module on. Open `/faculty/supervision` ("Students' work"): "The internships you supervise and the projects you guide."

1. "Internships" lists each one you supervise with its kind, dates, hours and window, the report, the parent's declaration for a family business, and the student's diary. Once the report is in, give the "Industry grade", the "Faculty visit on" and the "Viva marks", and press "Save". The credits are given last by the placement cell or the HOD, never by the supervisor.
2. "Projects" lists each project you guide. Under it give the "Day", the "Review", the "Marks" and tick "Final review, with the marks" when it is the last; press "Add the review". The final review carries the marks.

Internships are proposed by students and approved by the placement cell or the HOD, who name the supervising faculty member; you cannot approve one here.

## How do I keep my mentoring group?

Open `/faculty/mentoring` ("Mentoring"), or the "Mentoring" button on `/faculty`. Your HOD forms the groups each term; until then the page says "No mentoring group".

1. Each group card reads "Mentor {name} · {n} students" and lists the students, with a badge for any flag.
2. To add a note, choose the "Student", a "Flag" ("No flag", "Slow learner", "Advanced learner" or "Referred to counselling"), write "A note" and press "Add note".
3. The notes list below shows the last twenty, newest first.

The flags are the ones NAAC counts. Only the HOD forms or changes a group.

## How do I add my research to the register?

Only when the college has the IQAC module on. Open `/faculty/research` ("My research").

1. "My faculty record" shows what the office keeps about you (designation, qualification, API score, FDPs and publications), or "The office has not filled in your record yet."
2. Under "Research", choose the "Kind" ("Publication", "Patent", "Funded project", "Consultancy", "Book or chapter", "Conference paper", "Ph.D. scholar guided", "Award", "MoU" or "Seed money"), give the "Title", "Indexed in (publications)", the "Year", the "Amount in ₹ lakh (grants, consultancy)" if any, and a "Document (optional)". Press "Add to the register".
3. Press "Remove" beside one of your own items to take it off.

Your items are always your own; the IQAC desk can add an item in your name and read the whole college's register. Below the register the page shows the course feedback on the courses you teach, as totals only and only once five or more students have answered ("Fewer than five answers: the results stay hidden so no answer can be traced to anyone."). Under "Surveys for you" any teachers' survey the IQAC has opened is a form of 4-to-0 answers; press "Send my answers". A survey is answered once.

## How do I publish my public page?

Only when the college has the IQAC module on. Open `/faculty/profile` ("My public page"). The page is "A page about your work for the college's website, linked from its published disclosure: your name, designation, department, qualification, years of teaching and your research titles. Nothing else: no phone, no email. Only you put it up, and you can take it down at any time."

1. "What the page shows" is the card as it would read today, made from your faculty record and the research register.
2. Press "Publish my page". Later, "Refresh my page" republishes what the record says that day, and "Take my page down" removes it.
3. "Open the public page" opens the college's faculty list.

If the office has not written your faculty record yet, the page says so and offers "My research" instead; ask the office or the IQAC.

## How do I open the library's e-resources?

Only when the college has the Library module on. Open `/library/e-resources` ("E-resources"): "The journals, e-books and databases the library subscribes to. Each opens in a new tab, and the library counts the opens for its yearly return." Press "Open" beside a resource; it opens in a new tab. Open them through this page rather than from a bookmark, so the open is counted on the library's criterion-4 numbers. The librarian keeps the list (N-LIST, DELNET, e-journals, e-books and databases); until they have listed any the page says "The library has not listed any e-resources yet." Students see the same list on their own library page.

## How do I see what the head has written about me?

Open `/teacher/appraisals` ("My appraisal"): "What the head has shown you: observations, targets and appraisals, newest first." The "Timeline" lists each entry the head has marked visible to you; it is read-only, and nothing here is yours to write or answer. Until the head shares something it says "Nothing shared with you yet" and "When the head marks an observation, a target or an appraisal as visible to you, it shows here." The head writes these on your staff page and chooses, entry by entry, what you see; an entry not marked visible never appears. The page needs no module and is the same page a school's teacher reads.

## How do I apply for leave?

Open `/account/leave` ("Leave"). The balance card shows your leave by kind.

1. Press "Apply for leave".
2. Give the "First day away" and "Last day away", the "Kind of leave", "Why", and optionally "Who could cover your classes (optional)".
3. Send it. Your requests are listed below with their state; "Withdraw this request" takes one back, before or after it was approved.

Faculty leave is decided by the office or the principal, not by the HOD; the page's explainer says who answers at your college. The HOD arranges the cover for your lectures on the department's substitution board, and the colleague standing in is told.

## How do I check in and see my attendance?

Open `/account/attendance` ("My attendance"): "Your own days this month, as the office and your check-ins recorded them."

1. Press "I'm in" to record today. After the college's grace it says "Checked in — after {time}, so it is marked late."
2. The month's tiles count present, late, half and absent days and days on leave; an approved leave shows itself.

A second check-in in the day changes nothing, and there is nothing to check in to on a closed day.

## How do I read my appointment letters?

Open `/account/letters` ("My letters"): "Appointment letters the office has issued to you." Under "Letters", each is listed with its serial and the day it was issued; press its title to read it as it prints, or "PDF" to save it in the language it was issued in. A letter the office has withdrawn is marked "Revoked". Until the office issues one the page says "No letters yet". The office issues a letter from its certificates page, filled from your staff record; a detail the record lacks prints as a dash. No pay figure is printed on a letter, and only you and the office can open it.

## How do I write to someone, or post to the college's feed?

Open `/remarks` ("Messages") and press "New message" to write to anyone at the college; former students write to their faculty here too, and you answer in the same thread. The list is searched with "Search messages by name or subject" and narrowed with "Unread"; the heading counts the unread ones. Open `/bulletin` ("Social Media") to read the college's feed and press "New post" to share with the college, a programme, a department or a division; a post files under one category, and "Global feed" shows every school's and college's shared posts. A guardian reads the feed but cannot post; students' posts wait for review. Both pages are shared with every role and need no module.

## How do I manage my account?

Open `/account` ("Account"). Its tabs are "Sign-in and schools", "Preferences" and "Devices": your email and mobile, the colleges and roles you belong to (each row switches you there), the permanent record if you have one, notifications, language (English, Hindi or Marathi), appearance, and the devices signed in. When the college has the Payroll module on, "My payslips" is here too (your locked payslips, the structure you are paid on and your advances), and when it has the Inventory module on, "Things issued to me" (what the stores gave you and still hold against you, and your asks). The "Leave" card here opens the same leave page as the menu. A card offers to join another account of yours to this one, for someone who changed college and started again; and the college, not you, closes an account, by ending the membership, which the page explains.

## What are duties, and how do I open my desk?

A duty is a job the office gives you beside your teaching, from the row menu on its people page: lab in-charge, anti-ragging squad, NSS or NCC officer, research dean, exam flying squad, practice teaching coordinator, clinical coordinator or studio coordinator. The college's streams decide which are offered. When you hold one, its desk is in your menu under "Running things" and on the "My duties" card on `/faculty`; without the duty the page is not there. Each desk is described below.

### The lab in-charge's desk

Open `/duties/lab` ("Lab in-charge's desk"). "The labs" lists each room the office marked as a lab with its seats and, when the college keeps its stores in the app, the stock and assets kept there. "Batch rosters" lists the office's lab batches by division. Under "The practical record to sign", rows any teacher recorded wait for a signature: tick them and press "Sign the ticked rows", or "Open the practical record". Under "Breakage log", choose the "Lab", the "Item code", "How many broke" and "What happened", and press "Write it off"; the breakage is written off the lab's stock through the inventory, and the stock never goes below nothing.

### The anti-ragging register

Open `/duties/anti-ragging` ("Anti-ragging register"): the squad's rounds of the hostels as the warden logs them (when, which hostel, who went, "Something found" or "Nothing"), the complaints to the anti-ragging committee as "Filed on {date}, due by {date}" with their state ("Open", "Acknowledged", "Resolved" or "Sent to the ombudsperson") and "Overdue" when past the due day, and the committee's members. A complaint's words and who made it stay with the office and the principal; you read that it exists.

### The NSS and NCC units

Open `/duties/nss` ("NSS unit") or `/duties/ncc` ("NCC unit"). "Enrolled students" lists each volunteer or cadet with their unit, the day they joined and their hours this year ("Year met" at the NSS Manual's hours). To enrol, open "Choose students to enrol ({count} not enrolled)", tick them, give the "Unit or company" and press "Enrol"; "Leaves today" takes a student out of the unit from today. Under "Activities", give the "Activity", "Held on", "Hours each", tick "It was a camp" if it was, untick anyone under "Who took part" who did not, and press "Record the activity". Each activity is on the IQAC's activity register too.

### The research dean's desk

Open `/duties/research` ("Research dean's desk"): the college's whole research register, "By teacher" (publications, books and papers, projects and lakhs; the API scores are for the office and the principal) and "The register" newest year first. It is a read: each teacher adds their own items on "My research", and the office keeps the faculty record.

### The exam flying squad

Open `/duties/exam-squad` ("Exam flying squad"). "Papers" lists what is being sat and about to be, from two days ago to sixty days ahead, with their halls and the visits so far. To log a visit, choose the "Paper and hall", say "Who went", add a "Note", tick "Something was found (the exam controllers are told)" if it was, and press "Log the visit". "The squad's visits" lists them newest first, each "All in order" or "Something found". "Cases of unfair means" are as the exam cell entered them, "Waiting for the committee" or "Decided"; you read their state, never the note.

### Teaching practice

Only at an education college. Open `/duties/teaching-practice` ("Teaching practice"). Under "Practice schools" add a school with its "Practice school" name, "UDISE code" (eleven digits, if you know it) and "Contact", and press "Add the school". Under "Postings", choose the "Student teacher", the "Practice school", "From", "To" and "Lessons planned", and press "Post the student teacher". On a posting that has begun, record a lesson you watched with "Lesson on", a "Grade" and a "Note", and press "Record the lesson"; the observation is in your name. The tiles count the practice schools, this year's postings, lessons watched of planned, and how many student teachers meet the NCTE's weeks.

### Clinical and studio coordinators

Open `/duties/clinical` ("Clinical coordinator") or `/duties/studio` ("Studio coordinator"). Both say "Its records are coming": clinical postings and rotations, and design studios and their juries, are not kept in the app yet. Keep them as you do now.
