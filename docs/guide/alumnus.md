Roles: ALUMNUS

# The former pupil's guide (school and college)

This guide is for a former pupil of a school or college on Shalaghar. When you leave, the school keeps a place for you: you can read its feed, write to its staff, join its directory and ask for a paper again. You start at `/alumni`, the "Alumni" page. The bar carries "Alumni", "Social Media", "Messages" and "Alumni directory"; at a college it also carries "Your college". The "More" menu holds "Papers from the school", "Account" and "Alumni survey" (only at a college with the IQAC module on). "Social Media", "Messages" and "Alumni directory" are only in the menu while the school has the Alumni module on.

## What does my home page show?

Open `/alumni` ("Alumni"). The header reads "You left {school}, and the school keeps a place for you. You can read its feed and write to its staff.", with "Left {class} in {year}" under it. A line says what you may do on the feed: "You can read the school's feed.", "You can read the school's feed, react and comment." or "You can read the school's feed, comment, and share a post the office checks first." If the office has paused you, a banner reads "The school office has paused your messages and comments. You can still read the feed." with the office's reason.

1. Press "Read the school's feed" for `/bulletin`, or "Read every school's shared posts" for `/bulletin/global`.
2. Press "Write to the school's staff" for `/remarks`, "Open the alumni directory" for `/alumni/directory`, or "Your school record" for `/record`.
3. Press "Share a post with the school" when the school takes posts from its alumni.

"Your former teachers" lists the teachers who taught you and are still at the school, each marked "Taught you" with a "Message" button; "Write to someone else on the staff" opens `/alumni/staff`. "Notes from the school" lists what the office has written to its alumni, each marked "For the batch of {year}" or "For every alumnus".

## How do I write to a teacher or the office?

Open `/remarks` ("Messages"), or press "Message" next to a former teacher on `/alumni`. To reach somebody who did not teach you, open `/alumni/staff` ("Write to the school's staff"): "Your former teachers come first. A teacher can reply to you or close the conversation."

1. Type a name in "Search the staff" to narrow the list.
2. Press "Message" on the person (the button is titled "Write to {name}").
3. Write in the conversation and send.

A member of staff can close the conversation; it then shows "Closed" and "The school's staff closed this conversation. You can read it, and a member of staff can open it again." Too many new conversations in a day, or too many messages in an hour, are refused with "Try again in {minutes} minutes." While the office has paused you, the "Message" buttons are disabled.

## How do I share a post with the school?

Open `/alumni/share` ("Share a post with the school" on `/alumni`). It is there only when the school lets its alumni send posts; otherwise the page says "This school does not take posts from its alumni. You can still read the feed."

1. Give the post a "Title".
2. Write under "What would you like to share?".
3. Press "Send to the office".

The toast reads "Sent. The office will check it before it appears." Once the office approves it, the post appears on `/bulletin` ("Social Media") with your Alumni tag. On the feed itself you can react and comment when the school allows it, and "Comment" opens the post's thread.

## How do I join the alumni directory?

Open `/alumni/directory` ("Alumni directory"): "Former pupils who chose to be listed. The school's staff and its alumni can see it." Under "Your entry", the page says "Join if you would like the school's staff and other alumni to see where you are now. Nothing is shown until you join, and you can leave at any time." Under 18 it says "You can join the directory when you turn 18." instead.

1. Fill in "Town or city" and "What you do now".
2. Choose "How people may reach you": "Just list me", "Through the school office" or "Show my email address".
3. Press "Join the directory"; later, "Save my entry" changes it and "Leave the directory" removes it.

The toast reads "You are in the directory." or "You have left the directory, and what you wrote there is gone." Each entry shows "Batch of {year}", "Mentor" when the office has marked the person a mentor, and "Contact through the school office" where that was chosen.

## How do I ask for a paper again?

Open `/alumni/papers` ("Papers from the school"): "Ask for a paper again — a copy of your leaving certificate, a bonafide or a no-objection for higher studies, a transcript named in what it is for. The office may set a fee, paid at the office." "Certificates" lists each paper the school issued to you with "Download PDF".

1. Under "Ask for a certificate", choose the "Certificate" and say "What it is for".
2. Press "Send the request". The toast reads "Request sent. The office has been told."

"Your requests" lists each ask as "Waiting for the office", "Issued", "Declined" (with the office's reason) or "Taken back"; press "Take it back" on a waiting one. A fee the office sets shows on the line, and an issued paper shows "Open the certificate", which opens the PDF.

## How does somebody check that my certificate is real?

Anybody holding the paper opens `/certificates/check` ("Verify a certificate"), signed in to nothing. The foot of the certificate prints the address and the code.

1. Type the "Code" printed at the foot of the certificate.
2. Press "Check".

The page answers "This certificate is genuine.", "This certificate was issued but has since been revoked." or "No certificate carries that code.", with the kind, the serial, the school and the date. "Only the kind, serial, school and date are shown. The pupil's details are on the paper alone."

## What does my record and my account hold?

Open `/record` ("Your school record" on `/alumni`, or "Open it" under "Your school record" on `/account`): report cards, attendance and badges, kept whatever school you are at. Open `/account` ("Account") for "Sign-in and schools" (how you sign in, "Where you belong"), "Preferences" ("Notifications", "Language", "Appearance and reading") and "Devices" ("Signed-in devices"). "Closing your account" explains that an account is closed by asking the school; "Another account?" offers "Join them" for a login you had to start again. "Sign out" is at the foot.

## How do I answer the college's alumni survey?

Open `/alumni/survey` ("Alumni survey"; only at a college with the IQAC module on): "Tell the college how it prepared you. Your name is not kept with your answers." Answer the forms the college has opened. On `/alumni/college` a line reads "The college's alumni survey is open." with "Answer the survey" while one is open.

## At a college: how do I mentor, refer an opening and come to a meet?

Open `/alumni/college` ("Your college"): "What your college asks of its alumni: mentoring, referrals, meets and giving." At a school the page says "Not at a college".

1. Under "Mentoring", if the office has marked you a mentor, offer a free one-to-one: fill in "What it is" (for example "Mock interview — data analyst"), "Starts at", "Length" (30, 45 or 60 minutes) and "Your meeting link", and press "Offer the time". Each slot shows "free" or "booked"; after it, press "Held" or "Did not come". Otherwise the card reads "The office marks alumni as mentors. Ask the office if you would like to mentor students."
2. Under "Refer an opening", choose "Into a drive" (or "Not a drive here") or name "Or a company", write "The opening, and how to reach it", and press "Send"; the toast reads "Thank you. The placement cell is told." If you recruit, give "Your company" (its GSTIN or CIN as registered) and "Your role there" and press "Save".
3. Under "Meets and reunions", press "I will come" or "I cannot come" on each meet; the line counts "{count} coming".

"Gifts" lists what the college recorded of your gifts with "receipt {ref}": "Receipts are issued by the college, outside the app."

## What does Vidura answer for me?

Vidura is Shalaghar's assistant. Its one way in is the round button at the bottom right of every page, labelled "Ask Vidura" for screen readers; on a phone-width screen it sits above the bottom bar, and above the page's own round quick-action button where one is shown. The button is there only while Shalaghar's operator has Vidura switched on for the platform and for your school; the office does not switch it. There is no menu entry for Vidura, and opening `/assistant` takes you back to the page you came from with its drawer open.

Pressing the button opens Vidura's drawer. On a computer it docks at the right of the window and the page stays usable beside it, so you can follow the steps while you read them, and it stays open as you move between pages. On a phone it opens as a sheet from the bottom of the screen, and following a link from inside it closes it. Escape closes it, and so does "Close Vidura" (the cross).

For a former pupil it answers how to use Shalaghar: which page to open and the steps to take there. It does not answer topics from the textbooks, and it is not a general chatbot: anything else gets one sentence saying what it is for. Vidura knows the page it opened beside: "How do I use this page?" or "how do I do this here?" is answered from that page's own help and your own figures shown there. Under "My records", or when Vidura picks it, it answers questions about your own records from the pages you already see (your batch and last class, your certificate requests, unread messages), and under "Sources" it links the post or the message thread it read.

1. Type in "Your question" and press "Ask". Do not type names, phone numbers or anyone's details. An empty conversation shows "What Vidura is for" and, under "Try asking", three questions for your role and the page you are on (on a page other than your home the first is "How do I use this page?"); pressing one asks it.
2. To send one question a particular way, choose under "Mode for your next question": "Vidura picks" (the default), "Using Shalaghar" (how the platform works, which page and which steps) or "My records" (your own figures, counts and links, read from the pages you already see). The pick sends only your next question that way, then Vidura picks again, and no pick gets past a refusal. Each answer wears a small label naming the mode it was answered in.
3. Answer "Was this helpful?" with "Yes" or "No".
4. "New conversation" starts afresh. "Your conversations" lists the conversations of the last thirty days inside the drawer: press one to re-read it and ask more in it, "Delete this conversation" removes one sooner, and "Back to the conversation" returns.

Answers are set out with headings, numbered steps, bold for what matters, and a small table when your own figures have several rows. A definition is short (about sixty words) and a how-to gives numbered steps (up to about 250 words). If an answer stops at its length limit it says "The answer stopped at its length limit." and "Go on" continues it; "Go on" counts as one of your questions for the day, and an answer can be continued at most twice. Each person has twenty questions a day, a conversation remembers what was said in it only, and each conversation is deleted thirty days after it was last used.

When Vidura cannot answer (its one sentence saying what it is for, a refusal, or an answer saying it is not sure), the answer shows "Vidura could not answer this; a person at your school can." and one button: "Ask the school office" (at a college, "Ask the registrar's office"), which opens a message conversation with them. Nothing of your question is copied into the message; you write your own.
