Roles: DRIVER

# The driver's guide (school and college)

This guide is for a bus driver on the staff of a school or college on Shalaghar. You start at `/driver`, the "Transport" page, which is the only page in the bar. The "More" menu holds "Global feed" and "Assistant" under "Day to day", and "Account", "Leave" and "Staff attendance" under "Running things". The trips themselves are only there when the school has the Transport module on; otherwise `/driver` says the module is switched off.

## What do I see on the Transport page?

Open `/driver` ("Transport"). The header reads "Today's trips". The first time, a card titled "Your location during trips" explains: "While a trip is running, this phone sends the bus's location to the school every few seconds so families can see the bus. It is collected only between Start trip and End trip, and kept for thirty days."

1. Press "I agree" on that card once; the toast reads "Your location is sent only while a trip runs."
2. Read each trip card: "{bus}, {direction} trip" ("Morning", "Evening" or "Morning and evening"), "First stop at {time}", and its state "Waiting", "Live", "Ended" or "Missed start".
3. Read the stops under each trip and the roster at each stop, with "On the bus {time}" and "Off the bus {time}" against each pupil as they are ticked on the phone.

A note on the page says "Start and end trips from the Shalaghar app on your phone: it sends the bus's location while a trip runs." On the web you read the trips; starting a trip, ticking pupils on and off and ending it are done on the phone.

## What does the page say when nothing runs?

When the office has not put you on a bus and a route, `/driver` reads "No trips for you today" and "When the office puts you on a bus and a route, today's trips appear here." On a day the school is shut it reads "No school today." (or "No school today — {name}." on a holiday) and "No trips run on a day the school is shut." There is nothing to do on either day; the office keeps the buses, routes, rosters and drivers.

## How do I record that I am in?

Open `/account/attendance` ("Staff attendance"). The header reads "My attendance" and says "Late after {time}".

1. Under "Today", press "I'm in".
2. Read the toast: "Checked in. Have a good day." or "Checked in — after {time}, so it is marked late."

You can check in once a day. On a closed day the page says "The school is closed today; there is nothing to check in to." Below, the month's tiles and list show each marked day; "Previous month" and "Next month" move the view, and approved leave shows itself without a check-in.

## How do I apply for leave?

Open `/account/leave` ("Leave"). The header reads "Ask to be away. The office answers, and you see its answer here." The card "Leave this year" shows your entitlement and days used.

1. Press "Apply for leave".
2. Set "First day away" and "Last day away" and choose the "Kind of leave".
3. Write "Why" and press "Send to the office".

Each request lists as waiting, approved or declined with the office's note; "Withdraw this request" takes back a waiting one. The office, not the principal, decides a driver's leave.

## Where are the feed, my account and the assistant?

`/bulletin/global` ("Global feed") shows "Posts schools have chosen to share with everyone. Each one says which school published it, and each school decides for itself what to publish and what to show." `/account` ("Account") has three tabs: "Sign-in and schools" (how you sign in, "Where you belong", a "Leave" card with "Open", and "My payslips" with "Open" when the school has the Payroll module on), "Preferences" ("Notifications", "Language", "Appearance and reading") and "Devices" ("Signed-in devices"); "Sign out" is at the foot. Press "Ask the assistant" on any page or open `/assistant` ("Assistant"; only while the school has the Assistant module on): it answers how to use Shalaghar, which page to open and the steps to take there, and nothing else. Type in "Your question" and press "Ask"; do not type names or phone numbers.
