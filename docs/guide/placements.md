# The placement officer's guide (college)

This guide is for the training and placement officer at a college, whose home is `/placement` ("Placement"). The cell's pages are behind the Placement module; while the college has it off the home says so. The cell keeps the companies (each the same row on every college, by its GSTIN or CIN, checked by you before its requirement becomes a drive), the drives with their eligibility rule, applicants, rounds and offers, the students' records as the exam cell and the office hold them, the internships under the AICTE policy, the pre-placement talks, the soft-skills trainings, and the placement table the filings read. The menu holds "Placement", "Drives", "Students", "Companies", then "Day to day" with "Messages" and "Internships", and "Running things" with "Account", "Leave", "Staff attendance", "My letters" and "Placement table". Each cell page carries a row of tabs: "Season", "Drives", "Students", "Companies", "Internships", "Talks", "Training" and "Placement table". A recruiter is an outside account, not a member of the college; it reaches your college only through a live drive and sees a student's name only after that student applies. The office and the principal read the cell's pages; you write them.

## How do I read the season?

Open `/placement`. "The season of the batch of {batch}: who is placed, what is waiting, and every drive." The four tiles: "Final year" (students whose programme ends in the season's batch), "Placed", "Median salary" and "Placement index" (NBA: placed, higher studies and entrepreneurs over the final year). "Waiting for you" links "{count} requirements from recruiters", "{count} internships to approve" and "{count} companies to check". "Drives" lists the latest twelve with "Open a drive". When no student's programme ends in the batch the first tile says why it is 0 and that the season's batch is set under the placement table's settings.

## How do I set the season's batch and the dream ratio?

Open `/placement/analytics` ("Placement table") and scroll to "Settings".

1. Set "This season's batch" (the year of passing).
2. Set the "Dream ratio": "A placed student with the dream flag may apply again only when the drive pays at least this many times the accepted package."
3. Tick "Allow a drive limited by gender when a company insists" only if the college allows it; the default is not to.
4. Press "Save the settings".

"How these figures are counted" above states the rules in words: placed means holding an offer made and not declined, counted once at the best offer; one offer per student unless the dream rule applies; and whether gender-limited drives are allowed.

## How do I add a company and mark it checked?

Open `/placement/companies` ("Companies"): "Each company is the same row on every college, by its GSTIN or CIN. Check a company before its requirement becomes a drive."

1. Under "Add a company", give the "Company name", the "GSTIN or CIN" ("As registered, e.g. 27AAPFU0939F1ZV. Its form is checked; it is not looked up."), the "Point of contact (name, phone or email)" and tick its "Sectors". Press "Add a company".
2. A company already on the platform is linked to your college, not made twice.
3. In the companies table press "Mark checked" once you have satisfied yourself about it; it then reads "Checked". The columns show its identifier, sectors, "First came", "Drives" and "Alumni" (your college's alumni who work there).

A drive for a company nobody has checked goes no further than a draft, and a recruiter's requirement from an unchecked company cannot be made a drive. The check is the TPO's word, given once for every college.

## How do I turn a recruiter's requirement into a drive?

Open `/placement/companies`. "Requirements ({count})" lists what recruiters have sent your college: the role, the company (marked "not checked yet" if so), the package, locations, bond, rounds and "Dates proposed: {dates}". Press "Make it a drive" on one from a checked company; the drive opens as a draft with the requirement's details, and the requirement then shows "Open the drive". A second press opens the same drive. A recruiter sends a requirement to one college or several from their own account, and each college's cell reads it and decides; a requirement from a company nobody has checked keeps its button disabled until you "Mark checked" the company in the table below. Until a recruiter writes, the card says "No recruiter has written to your college yet." The drive then runs as any other: open it to students, invite, rounds, offers.

## How do I open a drive?

Open `/placement/drives` ("Drives") and press "Open a drive", which opens `/placement/drives/new`. "The drive opens as a draft; open it to students when it is ready. Who qualifies is computed from the rule." If no company is on your list yet the page says "No company yet".

1. Under "The drive": the "Company", the "Kind" ("On campus", "Off campus" or "Pooled"), the "Title", the "Venue", "Package, lowest (lakh a year)" and "Package, highest (lakh a year)" (the CTC as the company states it), the "Bond", the "First date (pre-placement talk or test)" and the "Job description".
2. Under "Rounds", choose up to four in order ("Aptitude test", "Technical test", "Group discussion", "Technical interview", "HR interview", "Coding test", "Case study", "Other"); "The shortlist comes before round 1."
3. Under "Eligibility rule": "Lowest CGPA", "Batch (year of passing)", "No live backlogs" (ticked by default), "Programmes (none ticked: all)" and, when the college allows it, "Only for" a gender.
4. For a pooled drive, tick the guest colleges under "Pooled with": "They apply from their own college; you see only those who consent."
5. Press "Open a drive".

The message says "The drive is open as a draft. Open it to students when it is ready."

## How do I run a drive from draft to offers?

Open the drive from `/placement/drives`. "About the drive" shows its company, kind, package, bond, venue, rounds, "Eligible" as "{eligible} of {total} students", the pooled colleges and the schedule.

1. Press "Move to: Open" when the company is checked. A status never goes back; an unchecked company's drive offers only "Move to: Closed".
2. Press "Tell the eligible students" on an open drive: only your college's eligible students are told, and those who applied are not told again. "Remind the applicants" nudges the live applicants before the next round.
3. Move on with "Move to: Shortlisting", "Move to: Rounds", "Move to: Offers" and "Move to: Closed". The rule is fixed once shortlisting starts; a closed drive changes no more.

"Who qualifies" lists every student of the college with their CGPA and live backlogs, "Eligible" or each failed condition in words (for example "CGPA {have} is below {need}", "{have} live backlogs; this drive takes none", "Already placed: a second drive needs the dream flag and at least ₹{need} lakh a year"). While the drive is a draft or open you may change "Lowest CGPA", "Batch (year of passing)" and "No live backlogs" and press "Save the rule". "Applicants" shows each applicant's shared record and where they stand; applying is the student's consent to share it with the company for this drive, and a minor's application waits for the guardian's consent.

## How do I enter a round's results?

Open a live drive. "Round results" says "One round at a time; round 0 is the shortlist. Leave a candidate blank to leave them out of this round. Each one is told."

1. Choose the "Round": "Shortlist (round 0)" or "Round {n}: {kind}".
2. For each candidate choose the "Outcome" ("Through", "Not through", "Absent" or "On hold"), or leave "Not in this round". Add "Feedback" and tick "Share the feedback with the student" to show it to them.
3. Press "Save the round".

Round n takes only those through round n − 1. A bound recruiter may enter the same results from their own account; the page shows the same list either way.

## How do I record an offer and a joining?

Open a live drive. "Record an offer" says "To a candidate still in the drive. A student who has accepted another offer receives this one only under the dream rule."

1. Choose the "Student", type the "Package (lakh a year)" and attach the "Offer letter (PDF or photo)".
2. If the figure is far above the drive's highest package, tick "The figure is right, even if it is more than {ratio} times this drive's highest package".
3. Press "Record the offer". The student is told and accepts or declines from their own placement page.
4. Once accepted, "Joining" lists the offer: give "Joined on" and press "Record".

The applicant's row shows the offer as "waiting for an answer", "accepted", "declined" or "joined", and "dream offer" where the dream rule applied. A bound recruiter may record offers and joinings too.

## How do I grant a student the dream flag?

Open `/placement/students` ("Students"): "The roll as the cell reads it: each record from the exam cell and the office, never typed here." Each student shows their programme and batch, CGPA, live backlogs, attendance, "This season" ("Opted out", "Higher studies", "Starting up", or "No profile yet" until they save a placement profile), their offers with the accepted package, and "Dream". Press "Grant" to give the dream flag; it then reads "Dream flag" and a second press withdraws it. With the flag a placed student may apply to a drive paying at least the dream ratio times their accepted package.

## How do I approve, follow and credit an internship?

Open `/placement/internships` ("Internships"): "Proposed by students, approved with a supervising faculty member, followed through the diary and the report, and credited last." The rule follows each student's programme, by the regulator the office stored for it: where the college has a programme under AICTE the card states the AICTE rule for credits and hours (after semester 2, 4 or 6, at most 20 credits); where it has a programme under any other regulator it says "Record the hours and the diary here; the credits follow the university's scheme for the programme.", and those students may propose an internship after any semester of their programme, credited up to the programme's total credits.

1. Each internship shows the student and company, its kind ("Industry", "Research", "Family business"; at a law college also court, law firm and legal aid), dates, hours, "after semester {n}", the supervisor, the "Report", the "Parent's declaration" for a family business, and "Diary, {count} days".
2. On a "Proposed" one, choose the "Supervising faculty" and press "Approve", or write the "Reason" and press "Refuse".
3. Once it reads "Report in", record the "Industry grade", "Faculty visit on" and "Viva marks"; the supervisor or the HOD may record these too.
4. Last, type the "Credits" and press "Save". It then reads "Credited" and "{credits} credits recorded for the exam cell."

Credits are refused until the report, the grade, the viva and any declaration are in ("Credits come last: the report, the industry grade and the viva first."), and never past the maximum of the student's rule (20 under an AICTE programme, the programme's total credits under another regulator). The department's HOD may decide and credit as you do.

## How do I set a pre-placement talk and mark who came?

Open `/placement/talks` ("Pre-placement talks"): "A company's talk to the students before its drive: who is going, and who came." A drive's page links "Set this drive's talk" to the same form.

1. Under "Set a talk", give the "Title", the "Company", the "Drive it belongs to" (one talk a drive, not a closed one, or "No drive"), "Starts", "Minutes", "Venue", "Seats" (empty for no limit) and a "Note for students". Press "Set the talk".
2. Students answer "Going" or "Not going" on the web and the phone until it starts; the list shows "Going" (of seats) and the status "Ahead", "Under way", "Held" or "Cancelled".
3. Once the talk has started, open it: under "Students", tick who came and press "Save attendance". "Tick who came and save; a student who answered and is not ticked is marked absent." Walk-ins on the roll may be ticked too.
4. Before it is held, "Cancel the talk" tells those going; a cancelled talk still ahead takes "Schedule again".

The first attendance mark fixes the talk as held, after which it can be neither moved nor cancelled.

## How do I run a soft-skills training?

Open `/placement/training` ("Soft-skills training"): "Courses in communication, aptitude, group discussion and interviews, with each session's attendance; a student completes one at its share of the sessions." The tiles count the trainings open for enrolment and "Students who completed one".

1. Under "Make a training", give the "Title", the "Skill" ("Communication", "Aptitude", "Group discussion", "Interviews", "Resume writing", "Teamwork", "Leadership", "Email etiquette" or "Other"), the "Trainer", "Seats", "Attendance to complete (%)" (75 unless you change it) and "What it covers". Press "Make the training".
2. Open it. Under "Sessions", add each with "Starts", "Minutes", "Topic" and "Venue" and press "Add the session"; "Cancel" a session with no marks, or "Bring back" a cancelled one.
3. Students enrol themselves while it is open; under "Students on the training", "Put students on it" and "Enrol those ticked" adds others, and "Take off those ticked" removes them.
4. Once a session has started, press "Mark this session", tick who came and press "Save the marks": "the rest are marked absent."
5. "Close enrolment" stops students enrolling; sessions and marks go on. "Open enrolment" opens it again.

Completion is counted from the attendance each time; a completed training shows on the student's placement profile.

## How do I publish the season's table for the filings?

Open `/placement/analytics` ("Placement table"). "The batches" gives three years of "Final year (N)", "Placed (X)", "Higher studies (Y)", "Entrepreneurs (Z)", the "Placement index", the "Median salary" and the offers per student; "NBA sheet", "NIRF sheet" and "The batch's offers" download as CSV. The cards below break the batch down by company, "Salary bands", "By programme", "By category" and "Recruiters by sector".

1. Under "Published seasons" ("The table as the cell published it, for the filings. Republishing replaces it."), set the "Batch (year of passing)".
2. Press "Publish the season".

The message says "The season of {batch} is published as it stands." The IQAC's predictor, the NBA and NIRF filings and the AICTE disclosure read the published rows; the IQAC reads this page too.

## What do alumni bring to the cell?

A former student's accepted offer stays on the drive that made it, so a closed season's drive still shows who was placed where, and the companies table counts your college's alumni at each company under "Alumni". Alumni who work at a company may refer an opening into one of your drives or name a company for the cell from their own college page; the office marks alumni as mentors who offer free one-to-ones and mock interviews to students. The cell reads these; it does not write the alumni pages.

## How do I use Vidura, and manage my own account?

Vidura is Shalaghar's assistant. Its one way in is the round button at the bottom right of every page, labelled "Ask Vidura" for screen readers; on a phone-width screen it sits above the bottom bar, and above the page's own round quick-action button where one is shown. The button is there only while Shalaghar's operator has Vidura switched on for the platform and for your college; the office does not switch it. There is no menu entry for Vidura, and opening `/assistant` takes you back to the page you came from with its drawer open.

Pressing the button opens Vidura's drawer. On a computer it docks at the right of the window and the page stays usable beside it, so you can follow the steps while you read them, and it stays open as you move between pages. On a phone it opens as a sheet from the bottom of the screen, and following a link from inside it closes it. Escape closes it, and so does "Close Vidura" (the cross).

For you it answers how to use Shalaghar, which page to open and the steps to take there, and your own records; topic questions from the books are for pupils, teachers, faculty and heads of department. It is not a general chatbot: anything else gets one sentence saying what it is for. Vidura knows the page it opened beside: "How do I use this page?" or "how do I do this here?" is answered from that page's own help and your own figures shown there. Under "My records", or when Vidura picks it, it answers counts from the cell's own pages (drives open, applicants, offers and students placed this season, companies), from the platform's own figures and never a list of students, and under "Sources" it links the post or the message thread it read.

1. Type in "Your question" and press "Ask". Do not type names, phone numbers or anyone's details. An empty conversation shows "What Vidura is for" and, under "Try asking", three questions for your role and the page you are on (on a page other than your home the first is "How do I use this page?"); pressing one asks it.
2. To send one question a particular way, choose under "Mode for your next question": "Vidura picks" (the default), "Using Shalaghar" (how the platform works, which page and which steps) or "My records" (your own figures, counts and links, read from the pages you already see). The pick sends only your next question that way, then Vidura picks again, and no pick gets past a refusal. Each answer wears a small label naming the mode it was answered in.
3. Answer "Was this helpful?" with "Yes" or "No".
4. "New conversation" starts afresh. "Your conversations" lists the conversations of the last thirty days inside the drawer: press one to re-read it and ask more in it, "Delete this conversation" removes one sooner, and "Back to the conversation" returns.

Answers are set out with headings, numbered steps, bold for what matters, and a small table when your own figures have several rows. A definition is short (about sixty words) and a how-to gives numbered steps (up to about 250 words). If an answer stops at its length limit it says "The answer stopped at its length limit." and "Go on" continues it; "Go on" counts as one of your questions for the day, and an answer can be continued at most twice. Each person has twenty questions a day, a conversation remembers what was said in it only, and each conversation is deleted thirty days after it was last used.

When Vidura cannot answer (its one sentence saying what it is for, a refusal, or an answer saying it is not sure), the answer shows "Vidura could not answer this; a person at your school can." and one button: "Ask the registrar's office", which opens a message conversation with them. Nothing of your question is copied into the message; you write your own.

Your own leave is at `/account/leave` ("Leave": "Apply for leave" with "First day away", "Last day away", "Kind of leave" and "Why"; the office or the principal decides), your check-in at `/account/attendance` ("I'm in"), your appointment letters at `/account/letters` ("My letters", each with a "PDF"), and `/account` holds your sign-in, language, appearance and devices. `/remarks` ("Messages", "New message") and `/bulletin` ("Social Media", "New post") are the college's messages and feed.
