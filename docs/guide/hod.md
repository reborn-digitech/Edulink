Roles: HOD

# The head of department's guide (college)

This guide is for a head of department at a college, whose home is `/hod` ("Department"). A head teaches too: your own lectures, courses, homework, internal marks, mentoring group, exam duties, research, public page, leave, attendance, letters and duties are the faculty member's pages, reached from "My teaching" in your menu, and the faculty guide describes each of them; this guide does not repeat them. What is yours alone is the department: who teaches what this term, the faculty load and the student ratio, the course files to review, the internal marks to release and lock, the defaulters and the condonations, the day's cover, the mentoring groups, the department's results, its projects and internships, its research and activities, the laboratory's line of the no-dues, the peer-team visit file, and at an engineering college the department's NBA report. The menu's "Day to day" holds "Messages", "My teaching", "Exam duties", "Projects and internships", "Homework", "Practical record" and "Assistant"; "Running things" holds "Account", "Leave", "Staff attendance", "My letters", "No-dues", "Peer-team visits", "Department research", "My public page", "My appraisal" and any duty's desk; "School life" holds "E-resources". A page behind a module is in the menu only while the college has that module on.

## How do I read the department's state?

Open `/hod`. The page is headed with your department's name and the current term. If you head no department yet it says "You head no department yet" and offers "My teaching"; the office names each department's head on its academics set-up page.

1. The four tiles: "Courses this term", "Course files behind", "Below the rule" (press it for the defaulters page) and "Condonations waiting" (press it for the condonations page).
2. "The department's courses this term" lists each offering with its "Faculty" ("No faculty yet" when unstaffed), "Coverage" (weeks taught of planned, "Behind" when two or more weeks behind the term), "Internal marks" (the parts, countersigned and released, or "Locked") and "Below the rule". A course code opens its course file.
3. "Load and the ratio" shows each faculty member's hours a week against the college's band (Under, Within or Over) and the department's students per faculty member against the norm.
4. "Lectures to cover on {date}" is the day's substitution board.
5. "Results and outcomes" and "Students' projects" sit side by side; the foot has "My teaching" and "Mentoring".

At an engineering college a card "NBA report of your department" offers "Open the report". If the office has given you a duty, "My duties" lists it with an "Open" link.

## How do I allot a course to a faculty member?

Open `/hod` and use the form under the courses table. It offers only your department's courses and faculty.

1. Choose the "Course" (shown with its semester), the "Division" or leave "Shared across divisions" for an elective, the "Faculty", the "Room" and the hours a week: "Lecture hours a week", "Tutorial hours a week", "Practical hours a week". "Capacity" is seats for an elective; blank means no limit.
2. Press "Offer it".

The offering appears in the table and on the faculty member's "My courses this term". The office puts its hours on the weekly timetable; lectures are made from the timetable when the faculty member first opens the day. An offering already made can be changed by the office on the academics pages.

## How do I arrange cover for a colleague who is away?

Open `/hod`. "Lectures to cover on {date}" lists today's lectures of your department whose faculty member is "On leave" or "Absent" on the staff register, and who stands in.

1. Beside the lecture, choose "Who stands in" from the free colleagues, or leave "Nobody yet".
2. Press "Save" beside it.

The one standing in is told, and the lecture appears under "Lectures on {date}" on their home marked "Standing in". The board is for today; "Nobody to cover" means no lecture of the day has its faculty member away. Faculty leave itself is decided by the office or the principal, not here.

## How do I review a course file?

Open `/hod`, press a course code, and read the "Course file" tab: the teaching plan with its coverage line, the resources and papers, and the outcome attainment. A file the plan has let fall two or more weeks behind the term is marked "Behind" and counted in "Course files behind".

1. At the foot of the tab the card reads "Not reviewed by the HOD yet." until you act.
2. Type "A note" and press "Mark reviewed".

The file then reads "Reviewed by {your name}: {note}" to its faculty member. You may also write the plan or add resources on a department course yourself; the same forms are open to you as to its faculty member.

## How do I release internal marks to students and lock them for the exam cell?

Open the course from `/hod` and choose the "Internal marks" tab. The card explains the order: "A colleague countersigns each part, the HOD releases it, and locks the marks for the exam cell."

1. A part shows "Countersigned by {name}" once a second member of the department has signed it; a part that reads "Not countersigned" cannot be released. You may countersign a part you did not enter and do not teach, as any colleague may.
2. Press "Release to students" beside a countersigned part. It is marked "Released" and appears on each student's internal marks page; a released part can no longer be entered.
3. When every part is in, press "Lock the internal marks". The page then reads "Locked by {your name}: no change is possible."

Locking freezes each student's internal total for the exam cell's results; every change after it is refused, including posting homework marks into a part. Lock a course only when its marks are final.

## How do I see the defaulters and tell them?

Open `/hod/defaulters` ("Defaulters"), or press the "Below the rule" tile on `/hod`. The page lists the students of your department below their programme's attendance rule and not condoned, with "Attended", "Percent", "Standing" and "Last notice". The card's heading counts "{n} below the rule" and says either "{n} told just now." or "Everyone here has been told this week."

Opening the page is the action: those not told this week are told (and the guardians an adult student allows), at most once a week per course. Nothing else is pressed. A student who is short asks for condonation from their own attendance page.

## How do I decide a condonation?

Open `/hod/condonations` ("Condonations"), or press the "Condonations waiting" tile on `/hod`. "Your department's asks: approve when the attendance is at or above the department's floor, forward to the principal below it, or refuse." The card "Waiting for you" lists the open asks; "Show every ask" shows decided ones too.

1. Each ask shows the student, the term, the course (or "All courses"), the ground ("Medical", "Sports", "NCC or NSS", "Cultural event", "Bereavement" or "Other"), the line "Attendance {percent} · the department's floor {floor}%", the student's note and "The document" they attached.
2. Write "A note" if you wish, then press "Approve", "Forward to the principal" or "Refuse".

The office sets the department's floor on the academics set-up page: at or above it you may approve; below it the ask goes to the principal with "Forward to the principal", and the principal decides. A condoned shortfall counts as eligible on the term's eligibility list, which the exam cell reads when a form is submitted. The student sees the state on their attendance page ("With the HOD", "Approved by the HOD", "With the principal", "Approved by the principal", "Refused").

## How do I form the mentoring groups?

Open `/faculty/mentoring` ("Mentoring"), or the "Mentoring" button on `/hod`. Below the existing groups the card "Form a mentoring group" says "A mentor and up to {max} students for the term."

1. Give the group a "Name", choose the "Term" and the "Mentor".
2. Tick the "Students in the group" (each shown with their programme code).
3. Press "Save the group".

No group may hold more than the college's students per mentor, which the office sets. "Results and outcomes" on `/hod` summarises "{groups} mentoring groups, {mentees} students mentored." Each mentor adds notes and flags on their own group; you read your own group's notes the same way.

## How do I read the department's results?

Open `/hod`. "Results and outcomes" reads "{term}: {passed} of {appeared} passed ({rate} %)." once the exam cell has published the term's results, with each course's pass rate, the toppers by SGPA and "Open backlogs". Until then it says "The department's results arrive when the exam cell publishes them." The figures are read from the exam cell's published run each time, never typed here, and count the department's own courses as well as the term's offerings, so an imported university result with no offering of its own is still in them. The pass rates by course, faculty and division for the whole college are the exam cell's analytics page, which the exam controller, the office and the principal read; ask them for a term's breakdown beyond your department.

## How do I set up a project and approve or credit an internship?

Only when the college has the Placement module on. Open `/hod/projects` ("Projects and internships"): "Students' projects with their guides and reviews, and the internships to approve, follow and credit."

1. Under "A new project", give the "Project title", choose the "Guide" from the faculty and tick the "Students"; press "Set up the project". A faculty member sets up the projects they guide themselves; you may set one up with yourself as guide.
2. "Projects" lists each with "Guide: {name}", its students, its reviews and its marks. On a project you guide, add a review with "Day", "Review", "Marks" and "Final review, with the marks", then "Add the review".
3. "Internships" lists every internship of the college. On a "Proposed" one, choose the "Supervising faculty" and press "Approve", or give a "Reason" and press "Refuse".
4. Once the report is in ("Report in"), record the "Industry grade", "Faculty visit on" and "Viva marks"; the "Credits" box is yours or the placement cell's alone, and comes last. Press "Save".

Credits are refused until the report, the industry grade, the viva and, for a family business, the parent's declaration are in, and never past the policy's maximum; at an engineering college the card states the AICTE rule. An internship credited within a term reaches that term's credit statement.

## How do I keep the department's research and activities?

Only when the college has the IQAC module on. Open `/hod/research` ("Department research"). "My faculty record" is yours as the office keeps it. "Research" is the department's register ("The department's items"), with the add form of the faculty guide; an item you add is your own. "Activities" is the department's activity register: choose the "Kind" (for example "Faculty development programme", "Workshop or seminar", "Extension activity", "Guest lecture", "Industrial visit"), give the "Title", the "Date", "Participants (head count)", the "Skill (skills programmes)" where it is one, "Staff who took part" and a "Document (optional)", and press "Add the activity". Below, the course feedback on the department's courses shows as totals from five answers up, and "Surveys for you" holds any teachers' survey.

## How do I clear the laboratory's line of a student's no-dues?

Open `/accounts/no-dues` ("No-dues"). "Before an exam form, on leaving the hostel and on leaving the college, every desk says the student owes it nothing. Each desk clears its own line; a new due reopens it."

1. Choose the event: "Exam form", "Leaving the hostel" or "Leaving the college".
2. "Still open for {event}" lists the students with a line open and the desks that hold it; press a student.
3. The student's lines show each "Desk" with "Owed", "Items out" and "Cleared" or "Still open". On the "Laboratory" line, which is the HOD's, write a "Note" and press "Clear this line".

Only the laboratory's line is yours; the accounts, library and hostel desks clear their own, and the office any. A line with money still owed or items still out needs the note to say why it is cleared. The exam cell refuses a form while a desk is open.

## How do I keep the department's peer-team visit file?

Only when the college has the IQAC module on, and for an NBA expert team's visit to your own engineering department; the IQAC makes the NAAC visits. Open `/hod/visits` ("Peer-team visits").

1. Under "A new visit", choose "For" NBA, give the "Title", the "Department", the "Academic year begins" (2026 for 2026–27), the "First day" and "Last day" (at most seven days) and "The visiting team" one a line as name, role. Press "Make the file".
2. On the visit's page, the "SAR pack" reads the department's SAR for the year from the filings with its state, hash and marks; "Not generated yet" names what is missing.
3. Under "Schedule", add a slot with its "Day", "From", "To", "What happens", "Where" and "Who takes it"; press "Add the slot".
4. Under "Evidence index", give the "Criterion or metric" (as 3.2.1), "What it is", what it "Points at" ("Filing", "IQAC file", "Metric's score", "NBA worksheet", "Link" or "Note") and the matching "Filing", "IQAC file", "Link" or "Note"; press "Add the line". A line whose target is gone reads "Missing".
5. "Download the index (CSV)" gives the team's table; "Mark the visit done" closes it.

The principal and the office read the file; you write your own department's NBA visits only.

## How do I write the department's NBA self-assessment report?

Only at a college that teaches engineering, with the IQAC module on, for the head of an engineering department; elsewhere the page says "Your department has no NBA report to write". Open `/hod/nba` ("NBA self-assessment report") from the "Open the report" button on `/hod`. "Write your department's qualitative criteria. The figures come from the records and the principal signs the report off."

1. Choose the "Year" and press "Show".
2. "From the records, as they stand" lists the computed figures; "Generating the filing freezes these figures with its fingerprint."
3. Under "The report's criteria", each item marked "From the records" is computed and cannot be typed; for the others give the "Self-assessed marks" (never above the item's marks) and "The department's account".
4. Press "Save the worksheet".

"Actionable insights: the NBA SAR" above the worksheet ranks which computed items are cheapest to raise and which self-assessed criteria still want writing; the platform does not score for NBA and every figure is the department's own records. The IQAC generates the report from `/iqac/documents` and sends it to the principal; once it is with the principal or signed off the worksheet is read-only.

## How do I try the department's figures in the SAR simulator?

Open `/hod/nba/simulator` ("NBA SAR simulator") from the insights on `/hod/nba`. "Change the department's figures and the self-assessed marks and see the SAR's total. Nothing is saved until you save a scenario." Change any figure under the computed items or any mark under the self-assessed criteria and the total recomputes in your browser; "Reset to today" puts the figures back. Under "Save as a scenario", give a "Name" and press "Save the scenario"; "Saved scenarios" lists your department's with their lead over today, each with "Load into the simulator", "Show as insights" and "Remove", and "Compare" sets two side by side. The college keeps at most a hundred scenarios.

## How do I keep the practical record of the department's courses?

Only at a college with a stream that keeps labs. Open `/hod/practicals` ("Practical record"). The page is the faculty guide's: "Courses with practicals" lists every course of your department offered with practicals, not only your own; choose one, and the sheet reads "Taught by {faculty} · {students} students · {experiments} experiments recorded" with each student's batch and a column per experiment. Record an experiment with "Record the experiment" (the "Experiment number", "Experiment", "Performed on" and "Students and marks") and sign rows under "To sign" with "Sign the ticked rows". The course's teacher or you as its head of department write; the teacher, you, or a member holding the lab in-charge duty sign; a signed row is final and is refused a change. A course with no practical hours or credits is not listed.

## Everything else I do as a teacher

Your own lectures and their registers, your courses' plans and internal marks, countersigning a colleague's part, homework and the assistant's switch on an assignment, your mentoring notes, your exam duties and a paper you are appointed to set, the internships you supervise and the projects you guide, your research items and public page, the e-resources, your appraisal, your leave, check-in, letters, account, messages and the feed, and every duty's desk (lab in-charge, anti-ragging squad, NSS and NCC, research dean, exam flying squad, teaching practice, clinical and studio) are the faculty member's pages. Open them from "My teaching" (`/faculty`) and the same menu entries, and follow the faculty guide's steps; the only differences are that a department course's internal marks and course file are also yours to release, lock and review, and that the "Homework" page lists the department's courses beside your own, each marked "Your department".
