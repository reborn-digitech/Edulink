## Full description (both stores, under 4,000 characters)

Shalaghar is your school's app. The register, marks, homework, report cards and messages from teachers — in one place, in your language.

FOR PARENTS AND GUARDIANS
• See whether your child was in school today.
• Which homework is due and which has been handed in.
• Exam marks and report cards, the moment the teacher issues them.
• Message the teacher directly, and book a time to meet.
• The school's notices and bulletin board.

FOR TEACHERS
• Take the morning register in a few taps.
• Enter exam marks paper by paper.
• Set homework, message families, apply for leave.

FOR PUPILS
• Today's timetable and what is still due.
• Practise a chapter at your own level and earn badges.
• Hand homework in straight from the app.

WORKS WITHOUT A SIGNAL
Network comes and goes. The register, marks and messages are saved on the phone and sent by themselves when you are back online — nothing is lost, nothing is sent twice.

THREE LANGUAGES
English, Hindi and Marathi. Switch whenever you like.

YOUR SCHOOL GIVES YOU THE SIGN-IN
Nobody creates an account in this app. Your school issues the sign-in to you or to your child. A parent links a child with the student code and the child's date of birth, or by accepting the school's invitation.

YOUR CHILD'S INFORMATION
No advertising, and no data sold to anyone. What the school records about a child is read by that school's staff who need it, the child's guardians and the child; the Shalaghar team, which runs the service, can open any school at any time, and the school is shown every visit. The app asks for the camera only to photograph work a pupil hands in, and for location only on a driver's phone, to show the school bus during a trip; no pupil's or parent's phone is located. Screenshots are blocked on the marks and report-card screens. What is kept, where it goes and for how long is in the full policy at PRIVACY_URL.

Shalaghar is for schools that have signed up. If your school is not on Shalaghar yet, ask the office to write to SUPPORT_EMAIL.

## Age rating notes

- Apple: expected 4+. Not submitted in the Kids Category. The app is used by pupils under a login their school issues, alongside their teachers and guardians; there is no advertising, no third-party analytics, and the only user-to-user channels are messages to school staff and a bulletin that staff review before a pupil's post is shown (policy rows 10–11).
- Google Play: IARC "Everyone" with the "Users Interact" descriptor expected. Target audience is a mixed audience that includes children under 13; the app is not enrolled in Designed for Families. See `privacy-answers.md`.
